Nikon System Messages Error Codes

Complete Nikon System Messages Guide

This guide explains all Nikon System Messages error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

BUSY
Camera Processing Data
  • Wait for the current operation to complete.
  • Do not turn off the camera during saving or processing.
  • Check if images are still buffering to the memory card.
  • Use a high-speed memory card for better performance.
LOCK
Control Lock Active
  • Check if the lock switch or function lock is enabled.
  • Disable control lock in the camera menu.
  • Hold the assigned lock button for a few seconds.
  • Restart the camera if controls remain unresponsive.
WAIT
System Processing Delay
  • Allow the camera to finish internal processing.
  • Avoid pressing buttons repeatedly.
  • Check if memory card write speed is sufficient.
  • Reduce burst shooting if delay persists.
ERR
General System Error
  • Turn the camera off and remove the battery.
  • Wait 1–2 minutes before restarting.
  • Check lens and memory card connections.
  • Update firmware if the error repeats.
RESET
System Restart Required
  • Power off the camera completely.
  • Remove battery for 1–2 minutes.
  • Reinsert battery and restart camera.
  • Restore default settings if issue persists.
SET ERROR
Settings Configuration Error
  • Reset shooting settings to default.
  • Check custom function settings.
  • Remove recently changed configurations.
  • Update firmware if available.
OVERHEAT
Temperature Warning
  • Turn off the camera immediately.
  • Allow the device to cool in a shaded area.
  • Avoid long video recording or burst shooting.
  • Ensure proper airflow around the camera body.
LOW TEMP
Low Temperature Warning
  • Warm the camera gradually before use.
  • Use insulated battery packs in cold environments.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes.
  • Keep spare batteries warm for better performance.
NO IMG
No Image Available
  • Insert a memory card containing images.
  • Check if files were deleted or moved.
  • Try another SD card.
  • Verify playback mode settings.
CARD ERR
Memory Card Error
  • Remove and reinsert the SD card.
  • Format the card in-camera.
  • Try a different compatible memory card.
  • Check for physical damage or lock switch.
FULL
Memory Card Full
  • Delete unnecessary images or videos.
  • Transfer files to a computer or storage device.
  • Use a higher-capacity SD card.
  • Format card after backup.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Nikon camera show a BUSY message?
Check if the camera is currently saving images or processing data. Wait for the operation to complete and avoid turning off the camera. Ensure your memory card has sufficient write speed for continuous shooting.
Why is my Nikon camera showing a LOCK message?
The LOCK message indicates that the control lock is active. Check the lock switch or settings menu and disable the lock feature. Restart the camera if controls remain unresponsive.
Why does my Nikon camera show RESET required?
The system may require a restart due to a temporary fault. Turn off the camera, remove the battery for a few minutes, then reinsert it and restart the device.
Why is my Nikon camera showing an OVERHEAT warning?
The camera temperature is too high. Turn it off immediately and allow it to cool in a shaded area. Avoid long continuous video recording or burst shooting.
Why does my Nikon camera display NO IMAGE?
No images are available for playback. Insert a memory card with photos or check if files were deleted or moved. Verify playback settings.
Why is my Nikon camera showing CARD ERROR?
The memory card may be damaged, incompatible, or improperly inserted. Remove and reinsert the card, then format it in-camera if necessary.
Why does my Nikon camera show LOW BATTERY warning?
The battery level is critically low. Replace or recharge the battery immediately. Turn off power-heavy features like Wi-Fi and LCD brightness to conserve power.
Why is my Nikon camera not responding (HANG/LOCKED)?
The system may be frozen due to processing overload or firmware issues. Remove the battery, wait a few minutes, and restart the camera.
Why does my Nikon camera show ERR SYSTEM message?
A general system error indicates a temporary malfunction. Restart the camera, remove external accessories, and reset settings if needed.
Why is my Nikon camera not saving images?
This may be caused by a full or corrupted memory card. Check storage space, format the card, and ensure write protection is disabled.
Why does my Nikon camera show BATTERY ERROR?
The battery may be incompatible or not properly seated. Clean battery contacts and test with an original Nikon battery if possible.
Why does my Nikon camera display FILE ERROR?
Image files may be corrupted or unsupported. Format the memory card after backup and ensure proper file handling.
Why is my Nikon camera showing SENSOR ERROR?
The image sensor may have detected a malfunction. Power cycle the camera and avoid impact or dust exposure. Service may be required if persistent.
Why does my Nikon camera show CONNECTION ERROR?
External accessory communication may have failed. Remove connected devices, clean ports, and reconnect properly.
Why do Nikon system messages keep appearing repeatedly?
Recurring system messages may indicate firmware issues, memory card faults, overheating, or internal hardware problems requiring diagnosis.
When should I bring my Nikon camera for repair?
Seek professional service if system messages persist after reset, the camera fails to operate normally, or hardware-related errors continue.
Important: If Nikon system messages continue after troubleshooting, stop using the camera. Persistent errors may indicate internal hardware failure, memory system damage, sensor malfunction, or motherboard issues. Continued use may worsen the condition. Contact an authorized Nikon service center for proper diagnosis and repair.
