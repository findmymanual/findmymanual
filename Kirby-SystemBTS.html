Kirby System Messages Error Codes

Complete Kirby System Messages Guide

This guide explains all Kirby System Messages error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

READY
System Ready for Operation
  • Device is functioning normally.
  • No action required.
  • Begin vacuum operation as needed.
STBY
Standby Mode Active
  • System is powered but not running.
  • Press power button to resume operation.
  • Check control settings if unresponsive.
RESET
Restart Required After Fault
  • Turn off and unplug the vacuum.
  • Wait 30–60 seconds before restarting.
  • Check if fault clears after reset.
LOCK
Safety Lock Engaged
  • Check safety latch and seals.
  • Ensure all parts are properly assembled.
  • Deactivate lock if procedure allows.
ERR
General System Error
  • Power cycle the unit.
  • Inspect attachments and connections.
  • Check for obstruction in airflow path.
OVERHEAT
Thermal Protection Activated
  • Turn off and allow unit to cool.
  • Clean filters and airflow passages.
  • Avoid extended continuous use.
  • Restart after cooling period.
HOT
High Temperature Warning
  • Stop operation immediately.
  • Check airflow and suction blockage.
  • Allow motor to cool before restarting.
SERVICE
Maintenance Required
  • Inspect belts, filters, and brush system.
  • Schedule professional servicing if needed.
  • Check for worn or damaged parts.
FILTER
Filter Cleaning Needed
  • Remove and clean filter thoroughly.
  • Replace filter if damaged or clogged.
  • Ensure proper airflow after cleaning.
BELT
Belt Wear Detected
  • Inspect drive belt condition.
  • Replace if cracked or loose.
  • Ensure proper tension after replacement.
BRUSH
Brush Roll Maintenance Needed
  • Remove hair and debris from brush roll.
  • Check brush rotation manually.
  • Replace brush roll if worn out.
AIR
Airflow Restriction Detected
  • Check hose and attachments for blockage.
  • Clean filters and dust container.
  • Ensure full suction path clearance.
CLG
Clog Detected in System
  • Disconnect hose and inspect for blockage.
  • Clear debris from airflow path.
  • Reassemble and test suction.
PWR
Power Supply Issue
  • Check outlet and power cord.
  • Inspect fuse or breaker.
  • Try a different power source.
SENS
Sensor Fault Detected
  • Inspect internal sensor connections.
  • Clean sensor area.
  • Replace sensor if unresponsive.
MTR
Motor Performance Issue
  • Check motor for obstruction.
  • Inspect wiring connections.
  • Replace motor if performance drops.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Kirby vacuum not respond?
Check power supply, control lock status, and system connection. Ensure the unit is properly plugged in and reset the vacuum system.
Why is my Kirby vacuum not starting?
Check power switch and electrical supply. Ensure attachments are correctly installed. Reset the system if the unit remains unresponsive.
Why is my Kirby vacuum not picking up dirt properly?
Inspect filter, dust bag, and hose for blockages. Check airflow path and remove any debris from attachments.
Why is my Kirby vacuum losing suction?
Check hose connections, filters, and dust container. Ensure there are no obstructions in the airflow system.
Why is my Kirby vacuum making unusual noises?
Inspect brush roll, belt, and internal components. Remove any debris or foreign objects inside the system.
Why does my Kirby vacuum stop mid-use?
Check for overheating protection, airflow blockage, or power interruption. Allow the unit to cool before restarting.
Why is my Kirby vacuum brush not spinning?
Inspect belt condition and brush roll. Check motor drive and replace worn belt if necessary.
Why is my Kirby vacuum hose clogged?
Remove hose and inspect for debris. Clear blockage using airflow or a cleaning rod.
Why is my Kirby vacuum attachment not working?
Check attachment compatibility and connection points. Ensure it is properly locked into the main unit.
Why is my Kirby vacuum overheating?
Check airflow system, clean filters, and remove blockages. Allow the vacuum to cool before restarting.
Why is my Kirby vacuum vibrating or shaking?
Inspect brush roll, belt alignment, and attachments. Remove any obstruction inside the cleaning head.
How do I reset my Kirby system messages?
Turn off power for 5–10 minutes, then restart the vacuum system to clear temporary faults and reset system messages.
Important: If Kirby system messages persist after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a certified technician.
