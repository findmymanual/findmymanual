Midea System Messages Error Codes

Complete Midea System Messages Guide

This guide explains all Midea System Messages error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

LOC
Control Lock Enabled
  • Press and hold the Lock button for 3–5 seconds.
  • Check for stuck or damaged control keys.
  • Refer to the user manual for unlock instructions.
  • Reset power if controls remain locked.
CL
Child Lock Activated
  • Disable Child Lock from the control panel.
  • Hold the designated lock button until cleared.
  • Inspect keypad for accidental activation.
  • Restart appliance if lock does not release.
KEY
Keypad Input Error
  • Clean control panel surface.
  • Check for moisture around buttons.
  • Press each key individually.
  • Replace keypad assembly if necessary.
ERR
General System Fault
  • Turn appliance off and disconnect power.
  • Wait several minutes before restarting.
  • Check for additional fault indicators.
  • Service appliance if message returns.
RESET
System Reset Required
  • Disconnect appliance from power.
  • Wait 5–10 minutes before reconnecting.
  • Restore settings after restart.
  • Monitor for recurring messages.
SYS
System Diagnostic Alert
  • Run appliance self-diagnostic if available.
  • Inspect all major functions.
  • Check control board connections.
  • Contact service if issue continues.
HOT
High Temperature Warning
  • Allow appliance to cool naturally.
  • Check ventilation and airflow.
  • Inspect cooling fans for operation.
  • Avoid heavy continuous usage.
COOL
Cooling Cycle In Progress
  • This is a normal operating status.
  • Allow cooling cycle to finish.
  • Do not disconnect power during operation.
  • No repair is required.
TEMP
Temperature Stabilizing
  • Wait for appliance to reach target temperature.
  • Avoid frequent door openings if applicable.
  • Ensure vents are unobstructed.
  • Monitor for additional alerts.
PF
Power Failure Detected
  • Verify household power supply.
  • Inspect breaker and outlet condition.
  • Reset appliance after power restoration.
  • Re-enter settings if necessary.
LOP
Low Power Supply Warning
  • Check incoming voltage stability.
  • Avoid overloaded electrical circuits.
  • Inspect power cord connections.
  • Use a dedicated outlet if possible.
HIP
High Voltage Protection
  • Disconnect appliance temporarily.
  • Verify household voltage levels.
  • Inspect surge protection devices.
  • Restart after voltage stabilizes.
COM
Communication Fault
  • Power cycle the appliance.
  • Inspect wiring between control modules.
  • Check connectors for corrosion.
  • Replace faulty control board if required.
NET
Network Connection Lost
  • Check Wi-Fi signal strength.
  • Reconnect appliance to network.
  • Restart router and appliance.
  • Update firmware if available.
CLN
Cleaning Recommended
  • Clean filters and air passages.
  • Remove dust and debris buildup.
  • Inspect moving components.
  • Reset maintenance reminder afterward.
SRV
Service Inspection Required
  • Perform basic troubleshooting checks.
  • Review any stored error messages.
  • Inspect electrical and mechanical systems.
  • Schedule professional service if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Midea appliance showing a Power Failure (PF) message?
A PF message indicates the appliance experienced a power interruption or voltage fluctuation. Check the household power supply, inspect the circuit breaker, and reset the appliance after power has been restored.
Why does the LOC message appear on my Midea appliance?
The LOC message means the control lock or child lock feature is active. Press and hold the designated lock button for several seconds to unlock the controls and restore normal operation.
Why is my Midea appliance displaying an ERR message?
An ERR message indicates the system has detected an operational fault. Restart the appliance, check for any additional error codes, and inspect for issues related to power, sensors, or controls.
What does the RESET message mean on my Midea appliance?
The RESET message indicates the system requires a restart to clear temporary faults. Disconnect power for several minutes, reconnect the appliance, and monitor operation after startup.
Why is my Midea appliance showing a HOT warning?
A HOT message means the appliance has detected elevated internal temperatures. Allow the unit to cool down, ensure proper ventilation, and avoid operating the appliance until temperatures return to normal.
What does the COOL message mean on my Midea appliance?
The COOL message usually indicates an active cooling cycle or cooldown process. This is generally a normal operating condition and the message should disappear automatically once the cycle is complete.
Why does my Midea appliance keep restarting by itself?
Unexpected restarts may be caused by unstable voltage, loose wiring connections, or a malfunctioning control board. Verify the power supply and inspect the appliance for recurring electrical issues.
Why is my Midea control panel locked or unresponsive?
A locked control panel may be caused by an activated child lock feature, moisture on the controls, or a stuck button. Unlock the controls, clean the panel, and perform a system reset if necessary.
Why does my Midea appliance display warning messages repeatedly?
Repeated warnings often indicate an unresolved problem such as overheating, sensor faults, communication errors, or unstable power conditions. The root cause should be identified and corrected to prevent recurring alerts.
Why is my Midea appliance not responding after a power outage?
Following a power interruption, the control board may require time to reboot. Disconnect power for several minutes, reconnect the appliance, and allow the system to initialize completely.
Why does my Midea appliance lose its settings after power is restored?
Loss of settings may occur after extended power outages or due to memory-related control board issues. Reconfigure the appliance settings and monitor for repeated occurrences.
Why is my Midea appliance showing multiple messages at the same time?
Multiple messages may indicate several system conditions occurring simultaneously, such as power interruptions combined with sensor or communication faults. Perform a complete reset and monitor for recurring messages.
How do I clear a Midea system message?
Turn off the appliance and disconnect power for 5–10 minutes. Restore power and verify normal operation. Temporary alerts often clear after a successful restart.
Why do Midea system messages keep returning after reset?
If messages return after resetting, the appliance may have a failing sensor, damaged wiring, overheating condition, communication fault, or defective control board requiring repair.
When should I call a technician for Midea system message issues?
Contact a qualified technician if warning messages persist after troubleshooting, repeatedly reappear, or are accompanied by performance problems, overheating, or power-related failures.
Important: If Midea system messages continue after performing basic troubleshooting, disconnect power if safe to do so and stop using the appliance. Persistent alerts may indicate electrical faults, communication failures, overheating conditions, sensor malfunctions, or control board problems. Continued operation may result in equipment damage or safety risks. Contact an authorized Midea technician for professional diagnosis and repair.
