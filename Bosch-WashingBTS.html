Bosch Washer Error Codes

Complete Bosch Washer Guide

This guide explains all Bosch Washer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

F02 / E17
Water Supply Issue
  • Check water tap is fully open.
  • Inspect inlet hose for blockage.
  • Clean inlet filter screen.
  • Verify household water pressure.
F17
Water Intake Timeout
  • Check inlet valve operation.
  • Ensure stable water supply.
  • Inspect hose for kinks.
  • Clean inlet filter.
F03 / E18
Drainage Failure
  • Check drain hose for blockage.
  • Clean pump filter.
  • Inspect drain pump impeller.
  • Remove debris from system.
F18
Drain Timeout
  • Inspect drain hose routing.
  • Check pump motor.
  • Clean lint and debris.
  • Restart cycle.
F16 / E16
Door Not Locked
  • Close door firmly.
  • Inspect latch mechanism.
  • Check wiring to lock system.
  • Replace door lock if faulty.
F34 / E27
Door Lock Failure
  • Check latch alignment.
  • Inspect locking motor.
  • Reset machine.
  • Replace lock system.
F21 / E21
Motor Failure / Drum Jam
  • Check drum for blockage.
  • Inspect motor brushes.
  • Remove load imbalance.
  • Replace motor if needed.
F01
Motor Triac Fault
  • Reset power.
  • Inspect motor wiring.
  • Check PCB triac.
  • Replace control board.
F23 / E22
Water Leakage Detected
  • Inspect hoses and seals.
  • Check detergent usage.
  • Look for water under machine.
  • Dry and restart system.
E23
Leak Sensor Activated
  • Check base tray for water.
  • Inspect internal seals.
  • Dry sensor area.
  • Restart machine.
E26
Water Pressure Sensor Fault
  • Check pressure switch hose.
  • Inspect wiring.
  • Clean pressure chamber.
  • Replace sensor if needed.
F57
Electronic Control Failure
  • Reset power.
  • Check PCB connections.
  • Inspect moisture damage.
  • Replace control board.
F29 / E28
Flow Sensor Error
  • Inspect flow sensor.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Clean inlet system.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E29–E32
Load Imbalance
  • Redistribute laundry.
  • Reduce load size.
  • Check drum suspension.
  • Restart cycle.
E36–E38
Pressure System Fault
  • Check pressure tubing.
  • Clean air chamber.
  • Inspect sensor system.
  • Replace sensor if needed.
E28
Low Voltage Error
  • Check power supply.
  • Avoid extension cords.
  • Inspect outlet stability.
  • Use voltage stabilizer.
E90
Control Board Communication Error
  • Reset power supply for 10 minutes.
  • Check internal wiring.
  • Inspect PCB board.
  • Replace control board if needed.
F19
Heating Timeout
  • Check heating element continuity.
  • Inspect NTC temperature sensor.
  • Verify wiring connections.
  • Replace faulty heating components.
F20
Unexpected Water Heating
  • Check temperature sensor operation.
  • Inspect heater relay on control board.
  • Verify heating circuit wiring.
  • Replace defective components.
F25
Turbidity Sensor Fault
  • Clean sensor area.
  • Inspect sensor wiring.
  • Check control board connections.
  • Replace turbidity sensor if necessary.
F27
Pressure Switch Fault
  • Inspect pressure switch hose.
  • Check for blockages.
  • Test pressure switch operation.
  • Replace faulty pressure switch.
F28
Flow Meter Failure
  • Inspect flow meter wiring.
  • Check water supply pressure.
  • Clean inlet components.
  • Replace defective flow meter.
F31
Overflow Condition
  • Turn off water supply immediately.
  • Check inlet valve for sticking.
  • Inspect pressure sensor system.
  • Drain excess water and restart.
F37
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Inspect NTC sensor wiring.
  • Test sensor resistance.
  • Check PCB connections.
  • Replace faulty temperature sensor.
F38
Temperature Sensor Short Circuit
  • Disconnect power.
  • Inspect wiring for damage.
  • Test sensor continuity.
  • Replace damaged sensor or wiring.
F42
Motor Speed Control Fault
  • Check motor tachometer.
  • Inspect drive motor wiring.
  • Verify PCB connections.
  • Replace motor or control board if needed.
F43
Motor Overload Protection Activated
  • Reduce laundry load.
  • Check drum for obstructions.
  • Allow motor to cool.
  • Restart wash cycle.
F44
Motor Rotation Fault
  • Inspect drive motor.
  • Check belt and drum movement.
  • Verify wiring connections.
  • Replace faulty motor components.
F59
3D Sensor Fault
  • Reset the washing machine.
  • Inspect sensor wiring.
  • Check vibration detection system.
  • Replace the sensor if defective.
F60
Flow Sensor Measurement Error
  • Inspect water inlet system.
  • Check flow sensor wiring.
  • Verify water pressure.
  • Replace faulty flow sensor.
F61
Door Lock Communication Error
  • Inspect door lock assembly.
  • Check wiring harness.
  • Reset the appliance.
  • Replace lock mechanism if necessary.
F63
Control Module Failure
  • Disconnect power for 10 minutes.
  • Inspect PCB for visible damage.
  • Check internal connectors.
  • Replace the control module.
F67
Control Board Coding Error
  • Restart the appliance.
  • Check firmware configuration.
  • Inspect control board connections.
  • Replace or reprogram the PCB.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Bosch washing machine not draining properly?
Check the drain hose for blockages or kinks. Clean the pump filter. Inspect the drain pump for debris or damage. Ensure the sink or outlet is not clogged.
Why is my Bosch washing machine not spinning?
An unbalanced load, overloaded drum, or drainage issue may prevent spinning. Redistribute clothes evenly and ensure the drain system is working properly.
Why is my Bosch washing machine not filling with water?
Check that the water supply tap is fully open. Inspect inlet hoses for kinks. Clean the inlet filter. Verify household water pressure is sufficient.
Why is my Bosch washing machine making unusual noises?
Loose items in the drum, unbalanced load, worn bearings, or foreign objects inside the drum can cause noise during operation.
Why is my Bosch washing machine leaking water?
Check door seal for damage, ensure detergent drawer is not clogged, inspect inlet and drain hoses, and verify connections are tight.
Why is my Bosch washing machine not starting?
Ensure the door is fully closed and locked. Check power supply. Disable child lock if active. Verify that a cycle has been properly selected.
Why does my Bosch washing machine stop mid-cycle?
This may be caused by water supply interruption, drainage issues, overload, or power fluctuations. Restart the cycle after checking conditions.
Why is my Bosch washing machine vibrating heavily?
An uneven floor, unbalanced load, or shipping bolts not removed can cause excessive vibration during spin cycles.
Why is my Bosch washing machine door not opening?
Wait until the cycle finishes and water drains completely. The door lock may take a few minutes to release after operation.
Why does my Bosch washing machine smell bad?
Detergent buildup, mold in the rubber seal, or stagnant water can cause odors. Run a drum clean cycle and leave the door open after use.
Why is my Bosch washing machine not heating water?
A faulty heating element, temperature sensor issue, or control board fault may prevent proper water heating during wash cycles.
Why is detergent not dissolving properly in my Bosch washer?
Low water pressure, cold wash settings, or blocked detergent drawer may prevent proper detergent mixing.
Why does my Bosch washing machine take too long to finish a cycle?
Unbalanced loads, low water pressure, or extended heating cycles can increase wash time significantly.
Why is my Bosch washing machine displaying an error code?
Error codes indicate issues with water supply, drainage, motor function, sensors, or electronic control systems. Note the code and follow troubleshooting steps.
How do I reset my Bosch washing machine?
Turn off the machine, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then restart. Select a new program to check if the issue is cleared.
When should I call a technician for my Bosch washing machine?
Call a technician if there are repeated error codes, burning smells, water leaks, or the machine fails to start or spin after troubleshooting.
Important: Always disconnect power before performing repairs. Contact a qualified technician if the issue persists.
