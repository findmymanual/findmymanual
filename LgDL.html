LG FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and solutions for LG washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, ovens, and appliances

General Troubleshooting
What does an error code mean on LG appliances?
Error codes indicate a detected fault in sensors, motors, drainage, heating elements, airflow systems, or control boards.
Should I reset my LG appliance when an error appears?
Yes. Power cycling or resetting may clear temporary faults, but repeated errors usually indicate a deeper hardware or system issue.
When should I call a technician?
Call a technician for burning smells, compressor or motor failure, repeated shutdowns, water leaks, or persistent error codes.
Why does my LG appliance stop mid-cycle?
This may be caused by overheating protection, power interruption, door lock issues, water supply problems, or sensor failure.
Washing Machine Issues
Why is my LG washing machine not starting?
Door lock issues, power supply problems, control panel faults, or child lock activation may prevent startup.
Why is my washing machine making loud noise?
Unbalanced load, worn bearings, loose drum components, or foreign objects inside the drum may cause noise.
Why is my washing machine not spinning properly?
Overloaded drum, motor issues, belt problems, or uneven laundry distribution may reduce spin performance.
Why is my LG washer leaking water?
Damaged door seal, clogged drain hose, or detergent overuse may cause leaks.
Refrigerator Issues
Why is my LG refrigerator not cooling?
Dirty condenser coils, faulty compressor, blocked vents, or defrost system failure may reduce cooling performance.
Why is my LG fridge making noise?
Fan motor issues, ice buildup, compressor vibration, or loose internal components may cause noise.
Why is water leaking inside my refrigerator?
Clogged defrost drain, damaged water line, or improper leveling may cause internal leaks.
Why is my freezer frosting up?
Door seal issues, frequent door opening, or defrost system failure may cause frost buildup.
Air Conditioner Issues
Why is my LG air conditioner not cooling?
Low refrigerant, dirty filters, compressor issues, or airflow blockage may reduce cooling efficiency.
Why is my AC leaking water?
Clogged drain pipe, improper installation, or frozen evaporator coil may cause leakage.
Why does my AC turn off by itself?
Overheating protection, unstable power supply, or sensor malfunction may trigger shutdowns.
Why is my LG AC making noise?
Loose fan blades, compressor vibration, or debris in the outdoor unit may cause noise.
Dishwasher Issues
Why is my LG dishwasher not draining?
Clogged drain hose, blocked filter, or faulty drain pump may prevent proper drainage.
Why are dishes still dirty after washing?
Blocked spray arms, low water pressure, or incorrect detergent usage may reduce cleaning performance.
Why is my dishwasher not starting?
Door latch issues, control lock activation, or power supply problems may prevent operation.
Why is my dishwasher leaking water?
Damaged door seal, overloading, or pump seal failure may cause leaks.
Electrical & Power Issues
Why does my LG appliance restart randomly?
Voltage instability, PCB failure, or power interruptions may cause random restarts.
Why does my LG appliance trip the breaker?
Short circuits, motor faults, or overloaded circuits may trigger breaker trips.
Can power surges damage LG appliances?
Yes. Power surges can damage control boards, compressors, motors, and sensors.
Why is my control panel not responding?
Moisture damage, wiring issues, or PCB failure may cause unresponsive controls.
Advanced Troubleshooting
Why do multiple error codes appear at once?
A single system failure such as PCB malfunction or power supply issues can trigger multiple related errors.
Can I fix LG errors myself?
Basic resets and cleaning are safe, but motor, compressor, and electrical repairs require a technician.
What is the most common LG appliance issue?
Washing machine drainage issues, refrigerator cooling failure, and AC airflow problems are most common.
Why does my appliance smell like burning?
Overheating motor, electrical failure, or damaged wiring may cause burning smells—stop use immediately.
Tip: LG appliances perform best with regular maintenance such as filter cleaning, proper loading, and stable power supply.
