Westinghouse Microwave Error Codes

Complete Westinghouse Microwave Guide

This guide explains all Westinghouse Microwave error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

F1
Touch Pad Fault
  • Check keypad ribbon cable connection.
  • Inspect control panel for moisture or damage.
  • Clean surface and remove debris.
  • Replace touch panel if unresponsive.
F6
Control Board Communication Error
  • Reset power by unplugging for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring harness connections.
  • Check for moisture on PCB.
  • Replace control board if failure persists.
F2
Overheat Protection
  • Allow microwave to cool down completely.
  • Check ventilation openings for blockage.
  • Ensure unit is not overused continuously.
  • Inspect thermal sensor or fuse.
F7
Thermal Fuse Triggered
  • Disconnect power immediately.
  • Check ventilation and airflow system.
  • Inspect internal temperature sensor.
  • Replace thermal fuse if required (technician recommended).
F3
Sensor Failure
  • Inspect temperature or humidity sensor wiring.
  • Check for loose or corroded connectors.
  • Test sensor resistance with multimeter.
  • Replace sensor if readings are abnormal.
F8
Humidity Sensor Error
  • Clean sensor surface inside cavity.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Test sensor response during operation.
  • Replace sensor if malfunction persists.
F4
Door Switch Error
  • Ensure door closes and latches properly.
  • Inspect door switch alignment.
  • Test door interlock switch continuity.
  • Replace switch if faulty.
dE
Door Not Closed Error
  • Close door firmly until latch clicks.
  • Check for food or debris blocking seal.
  • Inspect door hinges and alignment.
  • Replace latch if damaged.
F5
Magnetron Issue
  • Check high-voltage components carefully.
  • Inspect magnetron for burn damage.
  • Test diode and capacitor condition.
  • Replace magnetron if faulty (technician recommended).
H1
Heating Power Failure
  • Inspect high-voltage transformer.
  • Check capacitor and diode condition.
  • Verify power supply stability.
  • Replace faulty heating components if needed.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to reach proper operating temperature.
  • Check microwave environment stability.
  • Inspect sensor wiring and connections.
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Westinghouse microwave show system messages or error codes?
System messages usually indicate door switch faults, overheating protection, sensor failures, magnetron issues, control board errors, or power supply problems. Restart the microwave and inspect the affected components if the message returns.
Why does my Westinghouse microwave stop working suddenly?
Sudden stoppages may be caused by overheating protection, faulty door switch, unstable power supply, internal fuse failure, or control board faults. Turn off the microwave, unplug it for a few minutes, then restart after checking ventilation.
Why is my Westinghouse microwave not heating food properly?
Heating issues may be caused by a faulty magnetron, diode failure, capacitor problems, inverter issues, or incorrect power settings. Avoid running the microwave empty and ensure correct cooking settings.
Why is my Westinghouse microwave overheating?
Overheating is commonly caused by blocked ventilation, heavy continuous use, faulty thermal sensor, or cooling fan failure. Allow the unit to cool and ensure proper airflow.
Why does my Westinghouse microwave not start?
Ensure the door is fully closed and the latch is engaged. Check power supply, fuse condition, and control panel settings. A faulty door switch can prevent operation.
Why does my microwave show a door error?
Door errors occur when the latch system is misaligned, the door switch is faulty, or debris prevents proper closure. Close the door firmly and inspect the latch mechanism.
Why does my Westinghouse microwave show sensor errors?
Sensor errors may be caused by faulty humidity sensors, temperature sensors, wiring issues, or control board communication failures. Restart the microwave and inspect sensor connections.
Why is my microwave cooking unevenly?
Uneven cooking may result from faulty sensor calibration, weak magnetron output, incorrect power level, or turntable issues. Ensure food is properly positioned and rotate items when needed.
Why is my Westinghouse microwave making loud noises?
Unusual noises may come from the cooling fan, magnetron, turntable motor, or loose internal components. Stop operation and inspect if noise continues.
Why is steam or smoke coming from my microwave?
Steam may be normal during cooking, but smoke may indicate food residue, overheating, or electrical faults. Clean the interior and stop use if smoke persists.
Why does my microwave keep restarting or losing power?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, faulty PCB, loose wiring, or overloaded circuits. Check the outlet and avoid using extension cords.
Why does my Westinghouse microwave show a general error?
General errors usually indicate control board issues, temporary system glitches, or power interruptions. Perform a full power reset to clear temporary faults.
How do I reset Westinghouse microwave system messages?
Turn off the microwave, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power. This resets the control system and may clear temporary errors.
When should I service my Westinghouse microwave?
Service is recommended if heating fails, repeated error codes appear, door issues persist, unusual noises occur, or the microwave shuts down unexpectedly.
Important: Westinghouse microwave system messages are designed to protect key components such as the magnetron, high-voltage circuit, door switch system, cooling fan, sensors, and control board. If problems continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
