Cookie Policy

At Find My Manual, we value your privacy and transparency. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them when you visit our website Find My Manual.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, improve performance, and enhance your browsing experience. Cookies can be session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (stored for a period or until you delete them).

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes: Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly, enabling features like page navigation, checkout, and secure login. Performance and Analytics Cookies: Tools such as Google Analytics help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. Functionality Cookies: These remember your preferences (like device or region) to provide a more personalized experience. Advertising and Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant ads and track campaign performance through services like Google Ads or Meta (Facebook).

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party providers, including: PayPal – for secure payment processing Google – for analytics and advertising These third parties maintain their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.
