Haier Washer Error Codes

Complete Haier Washer Guide

This guide explains all Haier Washer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

ERR1
Door Lock Failure
  • Check door latch alignment.
  • Inspect door lock wiring connection.
  • Ensure door is fully closed.
  • Replace door lock assembly if faulty.
DL2
Door Not Closed Properly
  • Remove items blocking the door.
  • Check hinge alignment.
  • Inspect door seal condition.
  • Close door firmly and retry cycle.
ERR2
Drainage Problem
  • Clean drain pump filter.
  • Check drain hose for blockage or kinks.
  • Inspect pump motor operation.
  • Replace pump if not working.
DR3
Drain Timeout
  • Inspect drain hose for clog.
  • Check pump impeller for debris.
  • Ensure proper drainage height.
  • Restart cycle after cleaning.
ERR3
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Inspect thermistor wiring connection.
  • Test sensor resistance.
  • Check control board input.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
WL1
Water Level Sensor Error
  • Inspect pressure hose for blockage.
  • Clean air trap tube.
  • Check sensor wiring connection.
  • Replace sensor if needed.
ERR8
Overflow Protection Activated
  • Check inlet valve for stuck open issue.
  • Inspect pressure sensor system.
  • Verify water level inside drum.
  • Reset machine after draining.
HT1
Heating Element Failure
  • Test heating element continuity.
  • Inspect wiring harness.
  • Check relay output on control board.
  • Replace heater if faulty.
ERR6
Motor Malfunction
  • Inspect motor wiring connections.
  • Check drum for blockage.
  • Test motor resistance.
  • Replace motor if faulty.
MC7
Motor Communication Failure
  • Inspect inverter board connection.
  • Check communication cable.
  • Reset power system.
  • Replace PCB if needed.
UNB
Unbalanced Load Detected
  • Redistribute laundry evenly.
  • Reduce load size.
  • Check suspension system.
  • Restart spin cycle.
EEP
EEPROM Memory Error
  • Power reset machine for 10 minutes.
  • Check main PCB for damage.
  • Inspect firmware corruption.
  • Replace control board if needed.
SYS
System Communication Error
  • Reset washer power cycle.
  • Inspect wiring between modules.
  • Check for moisture damage.
  • Replace control board if required.
PF1
Power Failure During Cycle
  • Check household power stability.
  • Inspect circuit breaker or fuse.
  • Restart washing machine cycle.
  • Use surge protector if needed.
LE1
Water Leak Detected
  • Inspect inlet and drain hoses.
  • Check door seal for damage.
  • Look for water under machine base.
  • Replace leaking components.
IE1
Inlet Water Supply Error
  • Ensure water tap is fully open.
  • Clean inlet filter screen.
  • Check hose for blockage or kink.
  • Verify water pressure is adequate.
OE1
Drain Pump Performance Fault
  • Clean drain pump filter.
  • Check pump impeller for debris.
  • Inspect drain hose routing.
  • Replace pump if weak or silent.
UE1
Spin Speed Not Reached
  • Redistribute laundry evenly.
  • Remove heavy or single-item loads.
  • Check drum balance system.
  • Inspect motor and belt condition.
CE1
Control Panel Key Stuck
  • Clean control panel surface.
  • Check for moisture damage.
  • Inspect ribbon cable connection.
  • Replace keypad if unresponsive.
HE2
Heating System Overload
  • Allow machine to cool down.
  • Check heater relay condition.
  • Inspect wiring for overheating marks.
  • Replace heater assembly if needed.
TE1
Motor Tachometer Error
  • Inspect motor sensor wiring.
  • Check drum rotation resistance.
  • Test tachometer signal output.
  • Replace motor sensor if faulty.
SE1
Safety Switch Activation
  • Check lid or door switch system.
  • Ensure proper closure before operation.
  • Inspect locking mechanism.
  • Replace safety switch if needed.
CLN
Drum Cleaning Reminder
  • Run tub clean cycle with no laundry.
  • Use washing machine cleaner solution.
  • Wipe door seal and drum interior.
  • Leave door open after cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Haier washer overheating or stopping mid-cycle?
Ensure the washer is not overloaded and has proper ventilation around it. Avoid running multiple cycles continuously and allow the motor to cool down before restarting.
Why does my Haier washer take time to respond or enter wash mode?
This may happen due to system protection or heavy load detection. Reduce load size, restart the washer, and ensure stable power supply.
Why does my Haier washer stop during power fluctuations?
Unstable power supply can interrupt operation. Check outlet stability, avoid extension overload, and use a surge protector for safety.
Why does my Haier washer need to be reset frequently?
Frequent resets may indicate control board or software issues. Perform a full reset, check wiring connections, and avoid interrupting cycles.
Why is my Haier washer showing general errors or glitches?
This may be caused by sensor faults, drainage issues, or control board communication errors. Restart the washer and inspect connections.
Why does my Haier washer freeze or stop responding?
Too many operations or system overload can cause freezing. Turn off the washer, wait a few minutes, and restart the cycle.
Why does my Haier washer not start?
Check power supply, ensure door is fully closed, and verify that Child Lock is not enabled before starting a cycle.
Why is my Haier washer not spinning properly?
An unbalanced load or heavy laundry can affect spinning. Redistribute clothes evenly and restart the spin cycle.
Why is my Haier washer not draining water?
Check drain hose for clogs or bends. Clean the filter and ensure the drain pump is working properly.
Why is my Haier washer leaking water?
Inspect door seal, hoses, and detergent drawer for leaks. Ensure the washer is level and not overloaded.
Why is my Haier washer making loud noise?
Check for unbalanced load, loose items in drum, or worn bearings. Ensure the washer is placed on a flat surface.
Why is my Haier washer showing no signal or unresponsive display?
Check power connection, reset the washer, and inspect the control panel for faults or moisture damage.
Why is my Haier washer door not opening?
Wait until the cycle fully completes and water is drained. If locked, perform a reset or check door lock mechanism.
Why does my Haier washer restart by itself?
This may be caused by unstable power, faulty control board, or system protection. Check power source and reset the unit.
Why is my Haier washer slow or stuck on a cycle?
Blocked drain, heavy load, or sensor issues can slow operation. Clean filters and restart the washing cycle.
How do I reset my Haier washer?
Turn off the washer, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then plug it back in and restart the cycle to clear minor system errors.
Important: If the error code continues after troubleshooting, disconnect power immediately and contact a certified GE technician.
