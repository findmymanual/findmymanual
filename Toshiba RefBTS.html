Toshiba Air Conditioner Error Codes

Complete Toshiba Air Conditioner Guide

This guide explains all Toshiba Air Conditioner error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

F1
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check thermistor wiring connections.
  • Test sensor resistance with a multimeter.
  • Inspect terminals for corrosion or damage.
  • Replace the temperature sensor if defective.
F7
Freezer Sensor Failure
  • Inspect freezer temperature sensor wiring.
  • Check sensor resistance values.
  • Look for moisture or corrosion around connectors.
  • Replace the freezer sensor if faulty.
F8
Refrigerator Sensor Failure
  • Check refrigerator compartment sensor.
  • Inspect wiring harness connections.
  • Verify proper sensor operation.
  • Replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
F2
Defrost System Failure
  • Test defrost heater continuity.
  • Inspect defrost thermostat and wiring.
  • Check evaporator for excessive ice buildup.
  • Replace faulty defrost components.
H1
Defrost Heater Fault
  • Disconnect power before inspection.
  • Test heater resistance with a multimeter.
  • Check wiring connections to heater.
  • Replace heater if open circuit is detected.
F3
Fan Motor Error
  • Inspect evaporator fan for obstructions.
  • Check motor wiring connections.
  • Test fan motor operation.
  • Replace the fan motor if defective.
F9
Condenser Fan Failure
  • Clean dust from condenser area.
  • Check fan blade movement.
  • Inspect motor wiring and connectors.
  • Replace condenser fan motor if necessary.
F4
Compressor Issue
  • Check compressor relay and capacitor.
  • Inspect voltage supply stability.
  • Clean condenser coils.
  • Replace compressor or relay if needed.
C1
Compressor Start Failure
  • Verify incoming power supply.
  • Check compressor start relay.
  • Inspect overload protector.
  • Replace failed compressor components.
F5
Door Switch Error
  • Ensure the door closes completely.
  • Inspect door switch alignment.
  • Test switch continuity.
  • Replace door switch if faulty.
d1
Door Open Warning
  • Close refrigerator door securely.
  • Check door gasket for gaps.
  • Remove any items blocking closure.
  • Inspect door switch operation.
F6
Communication Failure
  • Power cycle the refrigerator.
  • Inspect wiring between control boards.
  • Check connectors for corrosion.
  • Replace the main PCB if communication fails.
E1
Control Board Communication Error
  • Disconnect power for 10 minutes.
  • Inspect all control board connections.
  • Check wiring harness integrity.
  • Replace defective control board if necessary.
I1
Ice Maker Fault
  • Verify water supply to the ice maker.
  • Inspect water inlet valve operation.
  • Check for frozen water lines.
  • Replace ice maker assembly if needed.
I2
Ice Tray Position Error
  • Inspect ice tray movement.
  • Remove any ice blockages.
  • Restart the ice maker system.
  • Replace motor assembly if defective.
P1
Power Supply Fault
  • Check wall outlet voltage.
  • Inspect power cord condition.
  • Verify circuit breaker status.
  • Contact an electrician if voltage is unstable.
PF
Power Failure Detected
  • Confirm household power has been restored.
  • Press any alarm reset button if available.
  • Monitor internal temperature.
  • Discard food if unsafe temperatures occurred.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Toshiba Refrigerator show error codes or warning messages?
Error codes usually indicate temperature sensor faults, fan motor failures, defrost system problems, communication errors, compressor issues, or door switch malfunctions. Reset the refrigerator and inspect the affected components if the code reappears.
Why is my Toshiba Refrigerator not cooling properly?
Check temperature settings, condenser coil cleanliness, airflow around vents, and door seal condition. Poor cooling may also result from fan motor, sensor, or compressor problems.
Why is my Toshiba Refrigerator freezing food in the fresh food compartment?
Inspect temperature settings and airflow controls. Faulty temperature sensors, control boards, or dampers may cause excessive cooling and freezing of stored food.
Why does my Toshiba Refrigerator make unusual noises?
Noises may come from evaporator fans, condenser fans, compressor operation, or ice buildup around moving parts. Check for obstructions and ensure the refrigerator is level.
Why is water leaking from my Toshiba Refrigerator?
Inspect the drain tube for blockages, check water line connections, and examine door seals. A clogged defrost drain is one of the most common causes of water leaks.
Why does my Toshiba Refrigerator keep beeping?
The refrigerator may beep when a door is left open, internal temperatures become too high, or a system fault is detected. Verify door closure and review any displayed error codes.
Why is ice building up inside my Toshiba Refrigerator?
Ice accumulation is often caused by a faulty defrost system, damaged door gasket, frequent door openings, or blocked air passages. Inspect the defrost heater and door seals.
Why does my Toshiba Refrigerator run continuously?
Continuous operation may result from dirty condenser coils, warm room temperatures, frequent door openings, low refrigerant levels, or faulty temperature sensors.
Why is the freezer not freezing properly?
Check freezer temperature settings, airflow, evaporator fan operation, and door seals. Defrost system failures and compressor issues can also reduce freezing performance.
Why does my Toshiba Refrigerator display a door error?
Door errors usually occur when the door switch is faulty, the door is not fully closed, or the gasket is damaged. Inspect the switch mechanism and ensure proper door alignment.
How do I reset Toshiba Refrigerator error codes?
Disconnect power for 5–10 minutes, reconnect the refrigerator, and allow the control system to restart. Temporary faults may clear automatically after a successful reset.
When should I service my Toshiba Refrigerator?
Professional service is recommended if cooling problems continue, the compressor fails to operate, excessive ice buildup returns, water leaks persist, or error codes remain after troubleshooting.
Important: Toshiba Refrigerator error codes and warning messages help protect critical components such as temperature sensors, fan motors, the compressor, defrost system, door switches, and electronic control boards. If problems continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
