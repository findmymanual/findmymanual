Sony Camera Error Codes

Complete Sony Camera Guide

This guide explains all Sony Camera error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E:62:10
SteadyShot (Image Stabilization) Error
  • Turn camera off and remove the battery.
  • Check for lens or sensor movement obstruction.
  • Reinsert battery and restart camera.
  • If error persists, service the stabilizer unit.
E:61:00
Autofocus Malfunction
  • Clean lens contacts carefully.
  • Reattach lens securely to mount.
  • Test with another compatible lens.
  • Service AF motor if issue continues.
E:91:01
Internal System Error
  • Power cycle the camera.
  • Remove memory card and retry.
  • Reset camera settings to default.
  • Update firmware if available.
C:32:60
Memory Card Error
  • Remove and reinsert SD card.
  • Format card inside the camera.
  • Try a different memory card.
  • Check SD card compatibility and speed class.
E:94:00
Lens Communication Failure
  • Clean lens electrical contacts.
  • Reattach lens firmly to mount.
  • Test with another lens if available.
  • Inspect lens mount for damage.
E:92:10
Shutter System Malfunction
  • Power cycle the camera.
  • Check for internal obstruction.
  • Test shutter in different shooting modes.
  • Service shutter mechanism if needed.
E5
Battery / Power Instability
  • Remove and reinsert battery.
  • Charge battery fully using original charger.
  • Clean battery contacts carefully.
  • Replace battery if performance is weak.
E:10:00
Power Supply Error
  • Turn camera off and remove battery.
  • Check battery or AC adapter connection.
  • Ensure power source is stable.
  • Restart camera after a few minutes.
E:62:11
SteadyShot Motor Failure
  • Restart camera system.
  • Check for lens stabilization movement blockage.
  • Remove and reattach lens.
  • Service stabilization unit if needed.
E:62:12
AF Drive Error
  • Turn off camera and reattach lens.
  • Clean lens mount contacts.
  • Test autofocus in manual mode.
  • Service AF drive system if failure continues.
C:13:01
Card Read/Write Failure
  • Remove SD card and reinsert.
  • Format memory card in camera.
  • Try a different SD card.
  • Check card speed and compatibility.
C:13:02
Unsupported Memory Card
  • Use compatible SD card type.
  • Check card specification requirements.
  • Format card in supported device.
  • Replace with recommended card model.
E:91:10
Shutter Stuck Error
  • Power off camera immediately.
  • Remove battery and reinsert.
  • Check for mechanical obstruction.
  • Service shutter unit if error persists.
E:92:20
Mirror / Mechanism Error
  • Restart camera system.
  • Avoid forceful shutter operation.
  • Check for internal blockage.
  • Seek professional repair if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Sony Camera show ERR or system error messages?
ERR messages usually indicate sensor issues, lens communication failure, memory card problems, shutter errors, or internal system faults. Turn off the camera, remove the battery, wait a few minutes, then restart. Check lens mounting, SD card condition, and ensure stable battery power.
Why does my Sony Camera stop working during shooting?
This is often caused by overheating protection, memory card write failure, battery instability, or internal firmware glitches. Turn off the camera, allow it to cool, remove the battery, and restart after a few minutes.
Why does my Sony Camera not turn on?
Check battery charge, battery contacts, and charger condition. Ensure the battery is properly inserted and try a different battery if available. Perform a reset by removing the battery for 5–10 minutes.
Why does my Sony Camera not focus properly?
Autofocus issues may be caused by dirty lens contacts, incompatible lens, low light conditions, or AF motor malfunction. Clean contacts, reattach lens, and test with another lens if possible.
Why does my Sony Camera produce blurry images?
Blurry images can result from slow shutter speed, incorrect focus mode, shaky hands, or lens stabilization issues. Use proper stabilization, adjust shutter speed, and check SteadyShot settings.
Why does my Sony Camera show overheating warning?
Overheating occurs during long video recording or high processing load. Turn off the camera, allow it to cool, and avoid direct sunlight or continuous recording for long periods.
Why does my Sony Camera not read memory card?
This is usually caused by incompatible SD cards, corrupted data, or dirty card contacts. Remove the card, clean it, format it in-camera, or replace it with a supported memory card.
Why does my Sony Camera show lens error?
Lens errors occur when the lens is not properly attached, contacts are dirty, or the lens motor is malfunctioning. Remove and reattach the lens and clean the contacts carefully.
Why does my Sony Camera show shutter error?
Shutter errors are caused by mechanical obstruction, wear in shutter mechanism, or internal system faults. Restart the camera and seek service if the issue persists.
Why does my Sony Camera drain battery quickly?
Fast battery drain may be caused by continuous video recording, Wi-Fi usage, old battery, or high screen brightness. Reduce usage features and replace battery if needed.
How do I reset my Sony Camera system messages?
Turn off the camera, remove the battery and memory card for 5–10 minutes, then reinsert and restart. This clears temporary system errors and resets internal settings.
When should I service my Sony Camera?
Service is recommended if lens errors persist, shutter malfunctions continue, autofocus fails repeatedly, or system error messages do not clear after reset.
Important: Sony Camera system messages are safety features designed to protect internal components such as the sensor, lens system, shutter mechanism, memory system, and processor board. If issues continue after troubleshooting, stop using the camera and contact an authorized Sony service center.
