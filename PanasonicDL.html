Panasonic FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

Common Panasonic appliance, air conditioner, TV, and device issues with solutions

General Troubleshooting
What does an error code mean on a Panasonic device?
Error codes indicate system-detected faults such as sensor failure, compressor issues, airflow blockage, or electrical communication errors.
Should I reset my Panasonic appliance when an error appears?
Yes. Power cycling may clear temporary glitches, but recurring errors require inspection.
When should I call a technician?
Call a technician if there are repeated errors, burning smell, compressor failure, or no power response.
Why does my Panasonic unit keep turning off?
This may be caused by overheating protection, unstable voltage, or faulty internal sensors.
Air Conditioner Issues
Why is my Panasonic AC not cooling?
Dirty filters, low refrigerant, blocked airflow, or compressor malfunction may reduce cooling performance.
Why is my Panasonic AC leaking water?
Clogged drain pipe, frozen evaporator coil, or improper installation may cause leakage.
Why does my AC make loud noises?
Loose fan blades, worn motor bearings, or debris inside the unit can create noise.
Why does my Panasonic AC smell bad?
Mold buildup, dirty filters, or stagnant moisture inside the unit may cause odors.
Why is airflow weak?
Clogged filters, weak blower motor, or dirty coils can restrict airflow.
Why does my AC turn on and off frequently?
Thermostat issues, voltage fluctuations, or sensor faults may cause short cycling.
Refrigerator Issues
Why is my Panasonic refrigerator not cooling?
Dirty condenser coils, faulty compressor, or blocked airflow may reduce cooling.
Why is my freezer too warm?
Defrost system failure, door seal leaks, or fan malfunction may cause temperature imbalance.
Why is there water inside my fridge?
Clogged defrost drain or blocked drainage line is a common cause.
Why does my fridge make clicking sounds?
Compressor relay or start capacitor issues may cause clicking noises.
Why is my fridge too noisy?
Faulty evaporator fan motor or compressor vibration may cause noise.
Washing Machine Issues
Why is my Panasonic washer not spinning?
Unbalanced load, lid switch failure, or motor belt issues may prevent spinning.
Why is my washer not draining?
Blocked drain hose, clogged pump, or faulty drain motor may cause drainage failure.
Why does my washer stop mid-cycle?
Power interruption, overheating motor, or control board failure may stop cycles.
Why does my washer vibrate heavily?
Unbalanced load or worn suspension parts may cause excessive vibration.
Why does my washer not fill with water?
Blocked inlet valve, low water pressure, or faulty solenoid valve may be the cause.
TV & Electronics Issues
Why is my Panasonic TV not turning on?
Power supply failure, damaged cable, or internal board issues may prevent startup.
Why is my TV screen black but has sound?
Backlight failure or display panel issues may cause this problem.
Why is my TV flickering?
Loose connections, faulty HDMI cable, or refresh rate issues may cause flickering.
Why is my remote not working?
Dead batteries, sensor obstruction, or remote pairing issues may be the cause.
Power & Electrical Issues
Why does my Panasonic appliance trip the breaker?
Short circuit, electrical overload, or internal component failure may cause tripping.
Can power surges damage Panasonic devices?
Yes. Surges can damage circuit boards, compressors, and sensors.
Why does my appliance randomly shut off?
Overheating protection or unstable power supply may cause shutdowns.
Sensor & Control Issues
Why does my Panasonic appliance show sensor errors?
Faulty temperature, humidity, or pressure sensors may trigger errors.
Why is my control panel not responding?
Moisture damage, loose wiring, or control board failure may cause unresponsive controls.
Can sensors be replaced?
Yes, most sensors can be replaced by a qualified technician.
Extra Common Questions
Why does my Panasonic appliance smell like burning?
Motor overheating, electrical faults, or belt friction may cause burning smell.
Why is there vibration during operation?
Unbalanced load, improper installation, or worn parts may cause vibration.
When should I replace instead of repair?
If repair costs are high or the unit is over 8–10 years old, replacement may be better.
Why is airflow weak in Panasonic AC?
Dirty filters, blocked vents, or weak fan motor may reduce airflow.
Tip: Regular maintenance, proper cleaning, and stable voltage supply help prevent most Panasonic appliance issues.
