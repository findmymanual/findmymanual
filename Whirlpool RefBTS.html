Whirlpool Oven / Range Error Codes

Complete Oven / Range Dryer Guide

This guide explains all Oven / Range Dryer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E0 / F0
Power Loss or Door Open
  • Check if power was recently interrupted.
  • Ensure refrigerator door is fully closed.
  • Inspect door switch sensor alignment.
  • Restart unit after power stabilizes.
PE
Power Supply Error
  • Check outlet voltage stability.
  • Inspect power cord and plug condition.
  • Reset circuit breaker.
  • Use dedicated outlet if possible.
PO
Power Outage Detected
  • Wait for stable power supply.
  • Reset refrigerator after outage.
  • Check surge protector condition.
  • Allow compressor delay restart (3–5 min).
PF
Power Failure Recovery Mode
  • Allow system to complete restart cycle.
  • Check for repeated voltage drops.
  • Inspect wiring connections.
  • Reset appliance if display freezes.
E1 / F1
Refrigerator Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check fridge thermistor connection.
  • Inspect wiring harness for damage.
  • Test sensor resistance.
  • Replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
E2 / F2
Freezer Sensor / Evaporator Fan Error
  • Inspect evaporator fan motor.
  • Check freezer temperature sensor.
  • Remove ice buildup around fan.
  • Test fan voltage supply.
E4 / F4
Defrost Sensor Fault
  • Check defrost sensor wiring.
  • Inspect evaporator coil area for frost.
  • Test thermistor resistance.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E7 / F7
Ambient Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check sensor placement inside cabinet.
  • Inspect wiring for corrosion or breaks.
  • Ensure proper airflow around unit.
  • Replace sensor if needed.
E8 / F8
Humidity / Cabinet Sensor Error
  • Inspect humidity sensor connection.
  • Check for moisture damage.
  • Clean sensor area.
  • Replace sensor if unresponsive.
E3 / F3
Evaporator / Condenser Fan Error
  • Inspect condenser fan motor.
  • Check evaporator fan operation.
  • Remove ice or debris blockage.
  • Test motor voltage supply.
DF
Defrost Failure
  • Check defrost heater continuity.
  • Inspect defrost timer/control board.
  • Clear ice buildup on evaporator.
  • Replace heater or sensor if needed.
E10
Defrost Heater Circuit Fault
  • Test heater resistance.
  • Inspect wiring harness.
  • Check relay on control board.
  • Replace heater if open circuit.
E5 / F5
Ice Maker Sensor Issue
  • Check water supply line.
  • Inspect ice maker module connection.
  • Clear ice blockage in mold.
  • Reset ice maker system.
E11
Ice Maker Motor Jam
  • Check for ice obstruction.
  • Inspect motor gears.
  • Reset ice maker module.
  • Replace motor if stuck.
E6 / F6
Communication Error
  • Reset refrigerator power for 10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring between control boards.
  • Check PCB connectors.
  • Replace main board if needed.
SY / CE
System Communication Failure
  • Check data cable between modules.
  • Inspect control board for damage.
  • Restart system power.
  • Replace PCB if fault persists.
E12
Control Board Sync Error
  • Reset system power.
  • Inspect wiring harness.
  • Check voltage stability.
  • Replace PCB if needed.
E9 / F9
Freezer High Temperature Alarm
  • Check door sealing condition.
  • Ensure compressor is running.
  • Avoid frequent door opening.
  • Allow system to stabilize temperature.
E13
Refrigerator High Temperature Warning
  • Check airflow inside fridge.
  • Inspect condenser coils.
  • Ensure proper door closure.
  • Reduce food overload blocking vents.
RST
System Reset Required
  • Unplug refrigerator for 5–10 minutes.
  • Reconnect power supply.
  • Allow compressor delay restart.
  • Monitor for recurring error codes.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Whirlpool Refrigerator show system messages or error codes?
Whirlpool refrigerator system messages usually indicate issues with temperature sensors, defrost system failure, evaporator fan problems, airflow blockage, door seal leaks, compressor issues, or control board communication errors. Restart the unit and check power supply, door sealing, and internal airflow if the message persists.
Why does my Whirlpool refrigerator stop working suddenly?
Sudden shutdowns may be caused by power interruptions, compressor overload protection, faulty control board, defrost system failure, or unstable voltage. Turn off the refrigerator, allow it to reset, then check power connection and breaker before restarting.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator not cooling properly?
Cooling issues may be caused by dirty condenser coils, faulty evaporator fan, low refrigerant, blocked air vents, or thermostat sensor failure. Clean coils, ensure proper ventilation, and check temperature settings.
Why is my refrigerator running but not cooling?
This usually indicates evaporator fan failure, compressor issues, or restricted airflow inside the freezer section. Check for ice buildup and ensure the fan is operating properly.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator temperature inaccurate?
Temperature inaccuracies are commonly caused by faulty thermistor sensors, wiring issues, or control board calibration faults. Test sensor resistance and replace if readings are outside normal range.
Why does my fridge show temperature warning or alarm?
Temperature alarms are triggered when internal temperature rises due to door left open, compressor failure, airflow blockage, or defrost system malfunction. Check door sealing and airflow first.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator icing up?
Excess ice buildup may be caused by defrost heater failure, defrost thermostat issues, or blocked airflow. Inspect evaporator coil and ensure defrost system is functioning.
Why is my refrigerator not defrosting?
Defrost failure is usually caused by a faulty heater, defrost sensor, or control board malfunction. Check heater continuity and replace defective parts if needed.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator fan noisy or not working?
Fan issues may be caused by ice obstruction, worn motor bearings, or wiring faults. Inspect evaporator fan and remove any ice buildup around blades.
Why is airflow weak inside my fridge?
Weak airflow is commonly caused by blocked vents, dirty fan, or frozen evaporator coils. Clear obstructions and ensure proper air circulation between freezer and fridge compartments.
Why does my Whirlpool refrigerator not start cooling after closing the door?
Ensure the door is fully closed and gasket is sealing properly. A faulty door switch or air leak can prevent proper cooling cycle activation.
Why does my refrigerator door alarm keep beeping?
Door alarms activate when the door is left open, not sealing correctly, or when the switch is faulty. Check gasket condition and door alignment.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator compressor not running?
Compressor failure may be caused by overload protection, start relay issues, capacitor failure, or control board faults. Check electrical components and allow system reset.
Why is my fridge clicking but not cooling?
Clicking sounds usually indicate a failing start relay or compressor unable to start. Inspect relay and capacitor for damage.
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator ice maker not working?
Ice maker issues may be caused by frozen water line, faulty inlet valve, or sensor failure. Check water supply and reset ice maker module.
Why is my refrigerator water dispenser not working?
This may be caused by clogged filter, frozen water line, or faulty inlet valve. Replace filter and ensure water line is not blocked.
Why does my Whirlpool refrigerator keep restarting?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, loose wiring, or control board issues. Avoid extension cords and ensure stable electrical supply.
Why does my refrigerator not turn on at all?
Check outlet power, breaker, fuse, and internal wiring. A faulty control board or power relay may also prevent startup.
Why does my Whirlpool refrigerator show a general system error?
General system errors are usually caused by control board communication failure or sensor interruption. A full power reset may temporarily resolve the issue.
Why is my refrigerator display not responding?
Display issues may be caused by control board failure, keypad malfunction, or wiring disconnection. Check connections and reset power.
How do I reset Whirlpool refrigerator system messages?
Turn off the refrigerator, unplug it or switch off breaker for 5–10 minutes, then restart. This resets the control system and clears temporary faults.
When should I service my Whirlpool refrigerator?
Service is recommended if cooling fails, ice buildup persists, compressor issues occur, error codes repeat, or the unit stops maintaining temperature.
Important: Whirlpool refrigerator system messages are designed to protect key components such as the compressor, evaporator fan, defrost system, thermistors, air ducts, water system, door seals, and control board. If issues continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
