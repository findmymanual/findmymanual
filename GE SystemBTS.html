GE System Messages

Complete Troubleshooting & Repair Guide

Control Lock Messages
LOCK
Control Lock Enabled
  • Press and hold the lock button for 3–5 seconds.
  • Refer to owner’s manual for unlock instructions.
  • Disconnect power briefly if controls remain locked.
  • Inspect control panel for stuck buttons.
Temperature Messages
HOT
High Temperature Warning
  • Allow appliance to cool completely.
  • Check ventilation openings for blockage.
  • Inspect cooling fan operation.
  • Avoid restarting until temperature normalizes.
COOL
Cooling Cycle Active
  • Wait for cooling cycle to finish.
  • Do not disconnect power during cooling.
  • Ensure proper airflow around appliance.
  • Restart appliance only after cycle completes.
System Messages
RESET
System Restart Required
  • Turn appliance OFF completely.
  • Disconnect power for 5 minutes.
  • Reconnect and restart appliance.
  • Check for recurring error codes.
ERR
General Appliance Error
  • Restart appliance power cycle.
  • Inspect for additional displayed codes.
  • Check wiring and connections.
  • Contact technician if issue persists.
Power Messages
PF
Power Interruption Detected
  • Check household power supply stability.
  • Inspect breaker and outlet condition.
  • Reset appliance after outage.
  • Use surge protection if outages are frequent.
Important: If the message continues after troubleshooting, disconnect power immediately and contact a certified GE technician.
