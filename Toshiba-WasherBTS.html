Toshiba Washer Error Codes

Complete Toshiba Washer Guide

This guide explains all Toshiba Washer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E1
Water Inlet Issue
  • Ensure water tap is fully open.
  • Check inlet hose for kinks or blockage.
  • Clean inlet filter screen.
  • Test inlet valve operation.
E9
Low Water Pressure Error
  • Check household water pressure.
  • Inspect inlet hose for blockage.
  • Clean inlet filter mesh.
  • Confirm water supply is stable.
E2
Drain Error
  • Inspect drain hose for blockage.
  • Clean pump filter thoroughly.
  • Check drain pump operation.
  • Replace pump if faulty.
ED
Drain Time-Out Error
  • Check for clogged drain hose.
  • Inspect pump impeller for obstruction.
  • Restart cycle after cleaning filter.
  • Replace drain pump if necessary.
E3
Door Lock Failure
  • Ensure door is fully closed.
  • Inspect door latch mechanism.
  • Check wiring to door lock.
  • Replace door lock if defective.
dL
Door Not Locked
  • Close door firmly until it clicks.
  • Check for clothing stuck in door seal.
  • Inspect door switch operation.
  • Replace latch assembly if needed.
E4
Motor Failure
  • Check motor wiring connections.
  • Inspect drum for obstruction.
  • Test motor resistance.
  • Replace motor if necessary.
LE
Motor Locked Error
  • Check for overloaded drum.
  • Remove tangled or heavy laundry.
  • Inspect motor rotor for blockage.
  • Reset washer and retry cycle.
E5
Water Overflow Detected
  • Check inlet valve for stuck open condition.
  • Inspect pressure switch operation.
  • Ensure drain system is not blocked.
  • Reset machine after fixing issue.
PE
Pressure Sensor Error
  • Check water level sensor hose.
  • Inspect pressure switch connections.
  • Clean air trap tube.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E6
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check thermistor wiring.
  • Test sensor resistance.
  • Inspect for corrosion or damage.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
tE
Water Temperature Sensor Error
  • Inspect temperature sensor connection.
  • Check wiring harness.
  • Test sensor with multimeter.
  • Replace sensor if reading is unstable.
E7
Heating Element Error
  • Test heating element continuity.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Inspect thermal fuse.
  • Replace heater if defective.
HE
Heating Circuit Fault
  • Check heater relay operation.
  • Inspect control board output.
  • Test thermal cut-off fuse.
  • Replace heating circuit components if needed.
E8
Unbalanced Load Detected
  • Redistribute clothes evenly in drum.
  • Avoid washing single heavy items alone.
  • Pause cycle and rebalance load.
  • Check suspension system if frequent.
Ub
Spin Imbalance Error
  • Open lid and redistribute laundry.
  • Remove bulky items causing imbalance.
  • Run spin cycle again.
  • Inspect shock absorbers if recurring.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Toshiba washing machine show system messages or error codes?
System messages on Toshiba washers usually indicate water inlet issues, drainage problems, door lock failure, motor faults, sensor errors, or control board communication issues. Restart the machine and inspect the affected components if the message returns.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine stop working suddenly?
Sudden stoppages may be caused by overload protection, unbalanced laundry, door switch failure, water supply interruption, or internal system errors. Turn off the washer, redistribute load, and restart the cycle.
Why is my Toshiba washing machine not filling with water properly?
Check that the water tap is open, inlet hose is not kinked, and inlet filter is clean. Low water pressure or faulty inlet valves can also prevent proper filling.
Why is my Toshiba washing machine not draining water?
Inspect the drain hose for blockage, clean the pump filter, and check the drain pump operation. A clogged or faulty pump is the most common cause.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine not start?
Ensure the door is fully closed and locked. Check power supply and control panel settings. A faulty door lock switch can also prevent starting.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine show a door error?
Door errors occur when the latch is not engaged, the door switch is faulty, or wiring is damaged. Close the door firmly and inspect the lock system.
Why is my Toshiba washing machine not spinning properly?
Check for unbalanced laundry, overloaded drum, or drainage issues. Motor faults or worn drive components may also reduce spin performance.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine stop mid-cycle?
Mid-cycle stopping may be caused by load imbalance, water supply issues, overheating protection, or control board faults. Restart after checking load and water conditions.
Why is my Toshiba washing machine overfilling or showing water level errors?
Inspect the pressure sensor tube, inlet valve, and drain system. A faulty water level sensor or blocked air tube may cause incorrect readings.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine leak water?
Check hose connections, door seal, detergent drawer, and drain pump area. Leaks are often caused by loose hoses or damaged gaskets.
Why is my Toshiba washing machine not heating water?
Heating issues may be caused by a faulty heating element, thermostat failure, or wiring problems. Inspect electrical connections and test heater continuity.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine show sensor errors?
Sensor errors are usually caused by faulty temperature sensors, pressure switches, or control board communication faults. Restart the washer and inspect sensor connections.
Why does my Toshiba washing machine keep restarting or losing power?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, loose power connections, faulty PCB, or overloaded circuits. Check the outlet and avoid using extension cords.
How do I reset Toshiba washing machine system messages?
Turn off the washer, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power. This resets the control system and clears temporary errors in many cases.
When should I service my Toshiba washing machine?
Service is recommended if error codes persist, drum does not spin, water does not drain, unusual noises occur, or the machine repeatedly stops mid-cycle.
Important: Toshiba washing machine system messages are designed to protect key components such as the motor, drain pump, inlet valve, sensors, door lock system, and control board. If issues continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
