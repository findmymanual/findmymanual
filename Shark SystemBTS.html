Shark System Messages

Status & Maintenance Alerts Guide

Status Messages
READY
Device Ready
  • Device is operating normally.
  • No action required.
  • Begin cleaning or use as intended.
System Control Messages
RESET
System Restart Required
  • Turn device off and unplug.
  • Wait 30–60 seconds before restarting.
  • Check if issue clears after reboot.
LOCK
Safety Lock Active
  • Ensure all parts are properly installed.
  • Check safety switches and latches.
  • Deactivate lock if applicable.
Thermal Protection Messages
OVERHEAT
Thermal Protection Triggered
  • Turn off device immediately.
  • Allow unit to cool completely.
  • Clean filters and airflow channels.
  • Avoid extended continuous use.
Maintenance Alerts
SERVICE
Maintenance Required
  • Inspect filters, brushes, and dust system.
  • Check for worn or damaged parts.
  • Schedule servicing if needed.
FILTER
Filter Cleaning Needed
  • Remove and clean filter thoroughly.
  • Replace if damaged or clogged.
  • Ensure proper airflow after cleaning.
Important: Regular maintenance helps prevent overheating and performance loss in Shark devices.
