Daikin Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and solutions for GE home appliances and system errors

General Troubleshooting
Why is my Daikin air conditioner not turning on?
Check the power supply, circuit breaker, remote batteries, and timer settings. A tripped breaker or loose wiring may also prevent startup.
Why does my Daikin AC keep turning off by itself?
This may be caused by overheating protection, dirty filters, unstable voltage, or faulty control board.
When should I call a technician?
Call a technician if there are refrigerant leaks, compressor failure, burning smell, or repeated error codes.
Why is my Daikin AC not responding to remote?
Check batteries, infrared sensor alignment, and possible obstruction between remote and indoor unit.
Error Code Issues
Why does my Daikin AC show U4 or U0 error?
U4/U0 often indicates communication failure between indoor and outdoor units or power supply issues.
Why does my Daikin display blinking lights?
Blinking lights usually indicate system protection mode, sensor failure, or abnormal operation detected by the unit.
Can I reset Daikin error codes myself?
Yes, you can try power cycling the unit, but persistent errors require professional servicing.
Why does my Daikin AC show multiple error codes?
Multiple errors may indicate control board malfunction or unstable electrical supply affecting multiple components.
Cooling & Performance Issues
Why is my Daikin AC not cooling properly?
Dirty filters, low refrigerant, blocked airflow, or compressor issues can reduce cooling performance.
Why is airflow weak from my Daikin AC?
Clogged filters, dirty evaporator coils, or fan motor issues may restrict airflow.
Why does my AC blow warm air?
Incorrect mode settings, refrigerant leak, or compressor failure may cause warm airflow.
Why does my Daikin AC take long to cool?
Dirty filters or undersized unit for the room can slow cooling performance.
Water & Drainage Issues
Why is my Daikin AC leaking water?
Clogged drain pipe, dirty filters, or frozen evaporator coil can cause water leakage.
Why is water dripping inside the room?
Improper installation or blocked drainage system may cause indoor dripping.
Why does my AC freeze up?
Low refrigerant or poor airflow due to dirty filters can cause icing on coils.
Noise & Odor Issues
Why is my Daikin AC noisy?
Loose fan blades, debris inside unit, or worn motor bearings may cause noise.
Why does my AC smell bad?
Mold buildup in filters or evaporator coil is a common cause of bad odor.
Why does my AC make clicking sounds?
Relay switching or expansion/contraction of internal parts may cause clicking noises.
Power & Electrical Issues
Why does my Daikin AC trip the breaker?
Electrical overload, short circuit, or compressor failure may trigger breaker trips.
Can power surges damage Daikin units?
Yes. Surges can damage PCB boards, sensors, and compressors.
Why does my AC randomly shut off?
Overheating protection or unstable voltage supply can cause shutdowns.
Maintenance & Prevention
How often should I clean Daikin filters?
Every 2–4 weeks depending on usage and environment conditions.
Why is regular maintenance important?
It improves efficiency, prevents breakdowns, and extends the lifespan of the unit.
Can dirty filters damage the AC?
Yes. Dirty filters reduce airflow and can cause freezing, overheating, or sensor errors.
Tip: Regular cleaning and proper installation help prevent most Daikin air conditioner issues.
