Shark Vacuum Cleaner Error Codes

Complete Troubleshooting & Diagnostic Guide

Brush Roll Errors
E1
Brush Roll Obstruction
  • Turn off vacuum before inspection.
  • Remove hair, threads, or debris from brush roll.
  • Ensure brush spins freely by hand.
  • Restart unit after cleaning.
Suction & Airflow Errors
E2
Suction Blockage Detected
  • Check hose and wand for clogs.
  • Clean filters and air pathways.
  • Empty dust container.
  • Restart vacuum after clearing blockage.
Motor & Thermal Errors
E3
Motor Overheating
  • Turn off vacuum and allow cooling.
  • Clean filters to improve airflow.
  • Avoid extended continuous use.
  • Restart after cooldown period.
Dust System Errors
E4
Dust Cup Not Installed
  • Ensure dust cup is properly attached.
  • Check locking mechanism.
  • Remove and reinstall securely.
  • Restart vacuum operation.
Filter Maintenance
E5
Filter Requires Cleaning
  • Remove and clean filter thoroughly.
  • Allow filter to dry completely.
  • Replace if worn or damaged.
  • Reinstall before use.
Power & Battery Errors
E6
Battery Fault or Low Charge
  • Charge vacuum fully before use.
  • Check battery connection points.
  • Replace battery if it no longer holds charge.
  • Use approved charger only.
Charging System Errors
E7
Charging Dock Communication Error
  • Check docking station power supply.
  • Ensure vacuum is properly aligned.
  • Clean charging contacts.
  • Restart both dock and vacuum.
System Errors
E8
Internal System Failure
  • Restart vacuum system.
  • Check for firmware updates if applicable.
  • Inspect internal wiring if accessible.
  • Contact service center if error persists.
Important: Always disconnect power before performing internal inspection or maintenance.
