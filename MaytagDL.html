Maytag Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and solutions for Maytag home appliances and system errors

General Troubleshooting
What does an error code mean on Maytag appliances?
Error codes indicate a detected fault in the system such as water flow issues, motor failure, sensor problems, or control board errors.
Should I reset my Maytag appliance when an error appears?
Yes. Power cycling can clear temporary glitches, but repeated errors usually indicate a hardware issue.
When should I call a technician?
Call a technician if there are repeated failures, burning smells, motor issues, or control board errors.
Why does my Maytag appliance stop mid-cycle?
This may be caused by lid switch issues, water supply interruption, overheating, or PCB failure.
Washer Issues
Why is my Maytag washer not spinning?
Unbalanced load, worn drive belt, or motor coupling failure can prevent spinning.
Why is my washer not draining?
Clogged drain pump, blocked hose, or faulty pump motor may be the cause.
What does F8 E1 error mean?
This indicates a water supply issue—either low water pressure or clogged inlet screens.
Why is my washer shaking violently?
This is usually due to uneven load distribution or damaged shock absorbers.
Why is my washer not filling with water?
Blocked inlet valve, kinked hose, or water supply issue may be responsible.
Dryer Issues
Why is my Maytag dryer not heating?
Faulty heating element, thermal fuse, or thermostat failure can stop heating.
Why is my dryer taking too long to dry clothes?
Blocked lint filter, clogged vent, or airflow restriction is the most common cause.
Why does my dryer stop mid-cycle?
Overheating protection or faulty door switch may cause shutdowns.
Why is my dryer making loud noise?
Worn drum rollers, belt issues, or foreign objects inside drum can cause noise.
Electrical & Control Issues
Why does my Maytag appliance keep restarting?
Power fluctuations or faulty control board can cause automatic restarts.
Why does my appliance trip the breaker?
Electrical short, motor failure, or overloaded circuit may be responsible.
Can power surges damage Maytag appliances?
Yes, surges can damage control boards and internal electronics.
Why is my control panel not responding?
This may be caused by moisture damage, PCB failure, or power supply issues.
Advanced Troubleshooting
Why do multiple error codes appear at once?
A single system failure (like PCB or power supply) can trigger multiple secondary errors.
Can I fix Maytag error codes myself?
Basic cleaning and resets are safe, but motor, sensor, and board issues require technicians.
What is the most common Maytag failure?
Drainage issues in washers, heating issues in dryers, and control board faults are most common.
Why does my appliance smell like burning?
This may indicate motor overheating, belt friction, or electrical short circuits—stop use immediately.
Tip: Regular cleaning of lint filters and drain systems prevents most Maytag appliance errors.
