How to Find Your Laptop's Model & Serial Number

Quick overview

Serial Numbers (S/N) and Model numbers help with warranty, support, and identifying compatible parts like ink, toner, and drivers.

  • The serial number is unique to your unit
  • The model number identifies the printer type and series

Common places to check

1
Bottom sticker

Most laptops have a sticker on the underside showing Model and Serial number.
Model: HP DeskJet 2710 and
S/N: CN12345678.

2
Under the battery

Some laptops hide the label inside the battery compartment.

3
Inside BIOS / system info

Turn on your laptop and press F2 / DEL / ESC to open BIOS, or check System Information in Windows.

4
Original box or receipt

The model and serial are printed on the barcode label.

Examples & formats

Serial and model numbers vary. Here are typical examples you might see:

    Model: Dell Inspiron 15 3000, HP Pavilion 14, Lenovo IdeaPad 3
    Serial:5CD12345AB, ABCD123456, PF1XYZ7890
👉 If you can’t tell which is which:
  • The model usually looks like a product name (brand + series)
  • The serial is longer, alphanumeric, and labeled S/N, SN, or Serial

Troubleshooting

  • Check underside carefully
  • Use flashlight for faded labels
  • Check system settings
  • Search purchase email

What to do with the numbers

  • Register warranty
  • Download drivers
  • Identify upgrades (RAM, SSD)
  • Contact support

🧠 Tip: Laptop serial numbers are usually 8–15 characters.
Models include brand + series.

🔒 Privacy & Safety Note

Serial numbers are unique identifiers. Do not post them publicly — share only with authorized support.

Quick tips

  • Labels are usually found on the Back, bottom, or Side Panels.
  • Some labels are behind removable Front Panels.
  • Keep a photo for easy reference when contacting support.
