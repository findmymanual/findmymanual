Philips Personal Care Device Error Codes

Complete Troubleshooting & Diagnostic Guide

Motor System Errors
E1
Motor Blocked
  • Turn off the device immediately.
  • Remove hair or debris blocking the motor.
  • Clean blades and moving parts thoroughly.
  • Restart device after cleaning.
Battery & Charging Errors
E2
Battery Low
  • Connect device to charger.
  • Allow battery to charge fully.
  • Use original Philips charger if possible.
  • Replace battery if runtime remains short.
E3
Charging Failure
  • Inspect charging cable and adapter.
  • Check charging port for dirt or damage.
  • Try another power outlet.
  • Replace charger or battery if needed.
Blade System Errors
E4
Blade Obstruction
  • Turn off and unplug device.
  • Remove blade attachment carefully.
  • Clean trapped hair or debris.
  • Reinstall blade securely before use.
Important: Regular cleaning and proper charging help prevent most Philips personal care device errors.
