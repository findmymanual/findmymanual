LG TV Error Codes & Troubleshooting

Complete LG Smart TV Problem Guide

No Signal
Check HDMI/Input Source
  • Ensure correct input source is selected.
  • Check HDMI cable connection.
  • Try another HDMI port.
  • Restart TV and device.
Boot Loop
Firmware or Software Issue
  • Unplug TV for 5–10 minutes.
  • Perform soft reset.
  • Disconnect external devices.
  • Update firmware if possible.
Black Screen
Backlight or Power Board Failure
  • Check power cable connection.
  • Increase brightness level.
  • Test with flashlight for image.
  • Contact technician if no display.
WiFi Error
Network Connection Issue
  • Restart router and TV.
  • Re-enter WiFi password.
  • Check network settings.
  • Move closer to router.
HDMI No Signal
Port or Cable Issue
  • Replace HDMI cable.
  • Try different HDMI port.
  • Check external device power.
  • Restart both devices.
Firmware Update Loop
Software Corruption
  • Disconnect internet connection.
  • Restart TV.
  • Retry update manually.
  • Factory reset if needed.
Audio Error
Speaker Board Issue
  • Check volume settings.
  • Test external speakers.
  • Restart TV.
  • Replace speaker board if faulty.
Remote Not Working
IR Sensor Failure
  • Replace remote batteries.
  • Check IR sensor line of sight.
  • Pair remote again.
  • Reset TV if needed.
App Not Available
Smart App Connection Issue
  • Check internet connection.
  • Update TV software.
  • Reinstall app if possible.
  • Restart TV.
Network Disconnected
Internet Connection Lost
  • Restart router.
  • Reconnect WiFi.
  • Check ISP status.
  • Reset network settings.
Update Failed
Firmware Update Failure
  • Check internet stability.
  • Retry update later.
  • Use USB update method.
  • Factory reset if needed.
Power ON Failure
Main Board or Power Supply Issue
  • Check power cable and outlet connection.
  • Try a different wall socket.
  • Unplug TV for 10 minutes and restart.
  • Contact technician if TV does not turn on.
No Picture
Backlight or T-Con Board Issue
  • Increase brightness settings.
  • Test screen with flashlight for faint image.
  • Check HDMI input source.
  • Replace backlight or T-Con board if needed.
Vertical Lines
Panel or Ribbon Cable Issue
  • Restart TV and check if lines persist.
  • Inspect HDMI input and cable.
  • Reset TV to factory settings.
  • Contact technician for panel inspection.
Frozen Screen
Software or Memory Issue
  • Unplug TV for 5–10 minutes.
  • Disconnect all external devices.
  • Perform soft reset.
  • Update firmware if accessible.
Apps Crashing
Software Corruption or Storage Issue
  • Clear app cache and data.
  • Update LG webOS software.
  • Reinstall affected apps.
  • Reset Smart Hub settings.
Sound but No Picture
Backlight Failure
  • Increase brightness to maximum.
  • Check screen with flashlight test.
  • Restart TV.
  • Replace backlight strips if needed.
Bluetooth Not Connecting
Wireless Module Issue
  • Turn Bluetooth off and on again.
  • Restart TV and device.
  • Remove paired devices and reconnect.
  • Update TV firmware.
Screen Flickering
Power or Panel Issue
  • Check HDMI cable and replace if needed.
  • Lower refresh rate settings.
  • Unplug TV and restart.
  • Inspect power board if issue continues.
Overheating Warning
Ventilation Issue
  • Turn off TV and let it cool.
  • Ensure proper airflow around TV.
  • Clean dust from vents.
  • Avoid blocking rear panel.
No Sound
Audio Output Issue
  • Check mute and volume settings.
  • Switch audio output mode.
  • Test external speakers or soundbar.
  • Reset sound settings.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my LG TV show “No Signal”?

This usually happens when the input source is incorrect or the HDMI cable is loose. Check the selected input, reconnect HDMI cables, and ensure the external device is powered on.

What does LG TV error code 137 mean?

Error 137 is commonly related to network or app connectivity issues. Restart your router, reconnect Wi-Fi, and restart the TV to resolve the issue.

What does LG TV error code 106 mean?

Error 106 indicates a network connection problem where the TV cannot reach the internet. Check your Wi-Fi settings and ensure stable connectivity.

Why is my LG TV not connecting to Wi-Fi?

This can be caused by router issues, incorrect password, or software glitches. Restart both the TV and router, then reconnect to the network.

What does LG TV error code 202 mean?

Error 202 usually indicates a DNS or internet connection failure. Try changing DNS settings or resetting network configuration on the TV.

Why does my LG TV keep buffering on apps?

Buffering is often caused by slow internet speed or unstable Wi-Fi. Use a wired connection if possible or restart your router.

Why is there sound but no picture on my LG TV?

This may be due to backlight failure or display settings issues. Try increasing brightness, resetting the TV, or checking for hardware problems.

Why does my LG TV keep turning off by itself?

This can be caused by sleep timer settings, power saving mode, or overheating. Check settings and ensure proper ventilation.

What does a blinking red light on LG TV mean?

A blinking red light usually indicates a power supply or internal hardware issue. Unplug the TV for a few minutes and restart it.

Why is my LG TV remote not working?

This may be due to low batteries, pairing issues, or sensor blockage. Replace batteries and re-pair the remote with the TV.

How do I fix LG TV apps not opening?

Apps may fail due to outdated software or corrupted cache. Update the TV firmware and reinstall the affected apps.

What does LG TV error “Unable to connect to server” mean?

This error indicates a temporary server or internet issue. Check your internet connection and try again later.

Why is my LG TV screen flickering?

Screen flickering may be caused by loose cables, refresh rate issues, or hardware failure. Check HDMI connections and update display settings.

How do I reset my LG TV?

You can reset your LG TV by going to Settings > General > Reset to Initial Settings. This will restore the TV to factory defaults.

What should I do if LG TV error codes keep appearing?

If errors persist after troubleshooting, update the firmware or contact LG support for hardware inspection.

Important: If issues persist, contact LG support or a qualified technician.
