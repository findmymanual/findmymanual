Samsung System Messages Error Codes

Complete Samsung System Messages Guide

This guide explains all Samsung System Messages error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

TO
Time-Out Error
  • Restart the appliance.
  • Check power stability.
  • Reduce load or task complexity.
  • Try again after reset.
OFF
System Shut Down
  • Verify power supply.
  • Check breaker or fuse.
  • Turn unit back ON after 5 minutes.
  • Inspect control board if issue persists.
RESET
Restart Required
  • Power cycle the device.
  • Wait 5–10 minutes before restarting.
  • Check for firmware updates.
  • Reset settings if needed.
BOOT
System Boot Failure
  • Disconnect power for 10 minutes.
  • Check main control board connections.
  • Inspect firmware corruption.
  • Replace PCB if needed.
PROTECT
Safety Protection Active
  • Allow unit to cool down.
  • Check ventilation and airflow.
  • Inspect for overheating issues.
  • Restart after safety reset.
PROTECT 1
Overcurrent Protection Active
  • Check power supply stability.
  • Inspect wiring for short circuit.
  • Unplug overloaded devices.
  • Reset system after inspection.
PROTECT 2
Thermal Shutdown Triggered
  • Turn OFF device immediately.
  • Let system cool for 30–60 minutes.
  • Clean ventilation fans and vents.
  • Restart after cooling period.
PROTECT 3
Overvoltage Protection Active
  • Check household voltage stability.
  • Use surge protector.
  • Inspect power cord condition.
  • Restart after voltage stabilizes.
CL
Child Lock Active
  • Press and hold lock button for 3–5 seconds.
  • Check manual for unlock combination.
  • Reset control panel if needed.
  • Restart device.
DOOR
Door Not Closed
  • Ensure door is fully closed.
  • Check for obstructions.
  • Inspect latch mechanism.
  • Clean latch area and retry.
INPUT
Invalid Input Detected
  • Check remote or keypad buttons.
  • Clean control panel surface.
  • Restart system.
  • Replace control board if needed.
ERR 01
System Startup Failure
  • Power cycle device.
  • Check main PCB connections.
  • Inspect firmware corruption.
  • Replace control board if needed.
ERR 02
Memory Corruption Detected
  • Reset system to factory settings.
  • Update firmware.
  • Check internal memory module.
  • Replace PCB if issue persists.
ERR 03
Boot Configuration Failure
  • Restart device.
  • Check firmware installation.
  • Perform system reset.
  • Reflash firmware if needed.
ERR 04
Sensor Initialization Failure
  • Check sensor wiring connections.
  • Inspect damaged sensors.
  • Restart system.
  • Replace faulty sensors if needed.
RESET LOOP
System Stuck in Reboot Cycle
  • Disconnect power immediately.
  • Wait 10–15 minutes before restarting.
  • Check firmware corruption.
  • Perform factory reset or service repair.
LOOP 1
Continuous Restart Detected
  • Unplug device for 15 minutes.
  • Check control board stability.
  • Inspect power supply fluctuation.
  • Replace PCB if necessary.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to reach proper freezing temperature.
  • Check freezer environment stability.
  • Ensure pint is pre-frozen correctly.
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Samsung System show ERR or system error messages?
ERR usually indicates a system fault such as sensor failure, communication error between boards, power fluctuation, or internal protection activation. Turn off the system, disconnect power or switch off the breaker for a few minutes, then restart. Check wiring connections and ensure stable power supply.
Why does my Samsung System stop working during operation?
This is often caused by overheating protection, airflow restriction, motor overload, or internal safety shutdown. Allow the system to cool, remove any blockages, and restart after a short break.
Why does my Samsung System not turn on?
Check the power outlet, breaker, fuse, and cable connection. Ensure stable voltage supply. If unresponsive, perform a full power reset by unplugging for a few minutes.
Why does my Samsung System lose performance or efficiency?
Reduced performance may be caused by clogged filters, blocked airflow, worn components, or sensor inaccuracies. Clean the system and ensure all parts are properly installed.
Why does my Samsung System make unusual noises?
Unusual sounds may come from loose components, debris inside moving parts, fan imbalance, or motor strain. Turn off the system and inspect all moving parts before restarting.
Why does my Samsung System smell like burning?
A burning smell may indicate motor overload, electrical overheating, or debris trapped inside the system. Stop use immediately, unplug the device, and allow it to cool before inspection.
Why does my Samsung System show overheating warning?
Overheating occurs when ventilation is blocked, airflow is restricted, or the system is used continuously for long periods. Turn off the unit, allow it to cool, and ensure vents are clear.
Why does my Samsung System shut down randomly?
Random shutdowns may be caused by unstable power supply, overheating protection, or internal board issues. Check power stability and restart after cooling.
Why does my Samsung System show blinking lights or warning indicators?
Blinking indicators usually signal system warnings such as sensor faults, door/connection issues, or protection mode activation. Restart the system and check connections.
How do I reset my Samsung System messages?
Turn off the system and disconnect power for 5–10 minutes. Restart the unit to clear temporary system errors and reset internal controls.
Why does my Samsung System freeze or become unresponsive?
This may be caused by software glitches, control board issues, or power interruptions. Perform a full power reset and ensure stable power supply.
When should I service my Samsung System?
Service is recommended if the system repeatedly shuts down, shows persistent error messages, makes abnormal noises, or fails to operate normally after troubleshooting.
Important: Samsung System messages are safety features designed to protect internal components such as motors, sensors, and control boards. If issues persist after troubleshooting, stop using the device and contact an authorized Samsung service center for repair.
