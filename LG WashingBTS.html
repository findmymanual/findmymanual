LG Washing Machine Error Codes

Complete LG Washer Troubleshooting Guide

UE
Unbalanced Load
  • Redistribute clothes evenly inside the drum.
  • Avoid washing single heavy items.
  • Check washer leveling.
  • Restart the spin cycle.
OE
Drainage Error
  • Inspect drain hose blockage.
  • Clean drain pump filter.
  • Check drain hose for kinks.
  • Restart the washer.
IE
Water Inlet Problem
  • Check water supply valves.
  • Inspect inlet hoses for bends.
  • Clean inlet filters.
  • Ensure proper water pressure.
FE
Overfill Error
  • Turn OFF water supply.
  • Unplug the washer.
  • Inspect inlet valve for leaks.
  • Contact service if issue persists.
PE
Water Level Sensor Issue
  • Restart the washer.
  • Inspect pressure sensor hose.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Replace faulty sensor if needed.
LE
Motor Lock Error
  • Turn OFF washer for 10 minutes.
  • Reduce load size.
  • Inspect motor wiring.
  • Restart wash cycle.
DE
Door Error
  • Ensure door is fully closed.
  • Inspect door latch for damage.
  • Remove trapped clothing.
  • Restart the washer.
CE
Motor Overload
  • Reduce laundry load size.
  • Turn OFF washer for cooling.
  • Inspect drum movement.
  • Restart after 30 minutes.
TE
Temperature Sensor Error
  • Disconnect washer power.
  • Inspect thermistor wiring.
  • Check heater assembly.
  • Replace faulty sensor.
SE
Hall Sensor Error
  • Turn OFF power.
  • Inspect motor sensor wiring.
  • Check rotor movement.
  • Replace hall sensor if necessary.
AE
Water Leakage Error
  • Inspect washer hoses.
  • Check door seal for leaks.
  • Dry excess water underneath unit.
  • Restart after inspection.
HE
Heater Error
  • Disconnect washer power.
  • Inspect heating element.
  • Check thermistor connections.
  • Replace faulty heater if needed.
E1
Water Supply Issue
  • Verify water faucets are ON.
  • Inspect inlet hoses.
  • Clean inlet screens.
  • Check water pressure.
E2
Drain Timeout
  • Clean drain filter.
  • Inspect drain hose blockage.
  • Check drain pump operation.
  • Restart the washer.
E3
Load Detection Error
  • Reduce load size.
  • Redistribute clothes evenly.
  • Restart the cycle.
  • Inspect load sensor if needed.
PF
Power Failure During Cycle
  • Check household power supply.
  • Restart washer cycle.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Inspect outlet voltage stability.
CL
Child Lock Active
  • Press and hold Child Lock button.
  • Refer to control panel instructions.
  • Wait for CL indicator to disappear.
  • Resume washer operation.
DL
Door Lock Issue
  • Inspect door latch mechanism.
  • Ensure door closes properly.
  • Restart washer power.
  • Replace door lock assembly if faulty.
SUD
Excess Detergent Detected
  • Use HE detergent only.
  • Reduce detergent amount.
  • Run rinse cycle.
  • Clean detergent residue.
FF
Frozen Drain Line
  • Move washer to warmer location.
  • Thaw frozen drain hose carefully.
  • Inspect for ice blockage.
  • Restart washer after thawing.
tE
Thermistor Error
  • Disconnect washer from power.
  • Inspect temperature sensor (thermistor) wiring.
  • Check connector for looseness or corrosion.
  • Replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
dE
Door Not Closing Properly
  • Ensure washer door is fully closed and latched.
  • Check for clothing stuck in door seal.
  • Inspect door strike and latch alignment.
  • Restart the cycle after closing properly.
CE
Current Error (Motor Overload/Drive Issue)
  • Reduce laundry load size.
  • Turn off washer for 10–15 minutes to cool down.
  • Check drum movement for obstruction.
  • Restart cycle after reset.
LE1
Motor Stalling Error
  • Turn off and unplug the washer.
  • Check for objects stuck in drum.
  • Inspect motor wiring and rotor.
  • Restart washer after inspection.
FE2
Water Overfill (Secondary Level Error)
  • Turn off water supply immediately.
  • Check inlet valve for proper closing.
  • Inspect pressure switch hose.
  • Call technician if water continues filling.
PE1
Pressure Sensor Calibration Error
  • Restart washer to reset sensor.
  • Inspect pressure hose for blockage.
  • Check wiring connection to control board.
  • Replace pressure sensor if issue persists.
IE2
Low Water Pressure / Fill Delay
  • Check household water pressure.
  • Ensure inlet valves are fully open.
  • Clean inlet filter screens.
  • Inspect hose for kinks or bends.
OE2
Partial Drain Blockage
  • Clean drain pump filter thoroughly.
  • Check drain hose height and placement.
  • Remove any lint or debris inside pump.
  • Restart drain cycle after cleaning.
UE2
Repeated Unbalanced Load Detected
  • Redistribute laundry evenly in drum.
  • Wash similar fabric weights together.
  • Reduce load size if overloaded.
  • Ensure washer is level on floor.
AE2
Water Leak Sensor Triggered
  • Inspect hoses for cracks or leaks.
  • Check detergent drawer overflow.
  • Dry base area under washer.
  • Restart after confirming no leaks.
PF2
Intermittent Power Supply Failure
  • Check wall outlet stability.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Reset circuit breaker if needed.
  • Restart washer cycle after stable power.

Frequently Asked Questions


What are common error codes in LG washing machines?

LG washing machines show error codes to indicate issues such as water supply problems, drainage failure, unbalanced loads, or motor faults. Most issues can be resolved with basic troubleshooting or a reset.

What does IE error mean on an LG washing machine?

IE indicates a water inlet problem. Check if the water taps are open, inspect the inlet hose for kinks, and clean the inlet filter screen.

What does OE error mean on LG washer?

OE means the machine is not draining properly. Clean the drain filter, check the drain hose for clogs, and ensure the pump is working correctly.

What causes UE error on LG washing machines?

UE indicates an unbalanced load. Redistribute clothes evenly inside the drum and avoid washing single heavy items alone.

What does LE error mean in LG washing machine?

LE indicates a motor lock or overload issue. Reduce the load, restart the machine, and check for drum obstruction or wiring issues.

What does DE error mean on LG washer?

DE means the door is not properly closed. Ensure the door is fully shut and check the door latch for damage.

What is PE error in LG washing machine?

PE indicates a pressure sensor error. This may require checking the water level sensor and its connections or replacing the sensor if faulty.

What does FE error mean on LG washer?

FE indicates water overfill. This may be caused by a faulty water inlet valve or pressure sensor issue.

What does TE error mean on LG washing machine?

TE refers to a temperature sensor (thermistor) error. Check wiring connections or replace the thermistor if needed.

What does CE error mean on LG washer?

CE indicates a current or motor overload issue. Reduce load, reset the machine, and inspect motor wiring if the issue persists.

What does PF error mean on LG washing machine?

PF means a power failure occurred during operation. Restart the cycle after ensuring stable power supply.

What does CL mean on LG washing machine display?

CL indicates Child Lock is activated. Press and hold the child lock buttons (varies by model) to disable it.

What is dE1 or dE2 error in LG washer?

dE1 or dE2 indicates a door lock issue. Check if the door is properly closed and inspect the door lock mechanism.

How do I reset an LG washing machine?

Turn off the machine, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power and restart. This can clear minor system errors.

What should I do if LG washing machine error codes keep coming back?

If error codes persist after troubleshooting, it may indicate a hardware fault. Contact an authorized LG service technician for diagnosis.

Important: If the error code continues after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified LG appliance technician.
