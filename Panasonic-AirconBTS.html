Panasonic Air Conditioner Error Codes

Complete Panasonic Air Conditioner Guide

This guide explains all Panasonic Air Conditioner error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

H11
Indoor–Outdoor Communication Error
  • Check wiring between indoor and outdoor units.
  • Inspect PCB connection terminals for looseness.
  • Restart power supply to both units.
  • Replace control board if issue continues.
H12
Capacity Mismatch Error
  • Verify correct indoor and outdoor unit pairing.
  • Check model compatibility.
  • Inspect PCB configuration settings.
  • Reconfigure or replace incompatible unit.
H13
Signal Transmission Error
  • Check signal wiring integrity.
  • Inspect for loose connectors.
  • Look for moisture damage on PCB.
  • Replace control board if needed.
H14
Indoor Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check indoor thermistor connection.
  • Inspect sensor wiring for damage.
  • Clean sensor housing area.
  • Replace sensor if readings are unstable.
H15
Outdoor Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Inspect outdoor sensor wiring.
  • Check sensor placement and exposure.
  • Clean dust or corrosion around sensor.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
H16
Pipe Temperature Sensor Error
  • Inspect pipe sensor wiring connection.
  • Check for loose terminals on PCB.
  • Verify sensor resistance value.
  • Replace sensor if abnormal.
H17
Current Overload Protection
  • Check compressor load condition.
  • Inspect voltage stability.
  • Clean condenser coils.
  • Service compressor if overload persists.
H18
Power Supply Abnormality
  • Check main power source stability.
  • Inspect circuit breaker condition.
  • Avoid extension cords.
  • Reset unit after stable power returns.
H19
Fan Motor Lock Error
  • Check fan blades for obstruction.
  • Inspect motor wiring and capacitor.
  • Remove dust buildup from fan assembly.
  • Replace fan motor if seized.
H20
Indoor Fan Motor Fault
  • Inspect indoor fan motor operation.
  • Check wiring and connector pins.
  • Clean blower wheel and housing.
  • Replace motor if not rotating.
H21
Outdoor Fan Motor Fault
  • Inspect outdoor fan blade movement.
  • Check capacitor condition.
  • Remove debris around fan area.
  • Replace motor if required.
H22
Heat Exchanger Temperature Abnormal
  • Check refrigerant level.
  • Inspect coil cleanliness.
  • Clean indoor and outdoor coils.
  • Ensure proper airflow.
H23
Evaporator Freezing Protection
  • Check airflow restriction.
  • Clean air filters thoroughly.
  • Increase fan speed if needed.
  • Allow ice to melt before restart.
H24
Outdoor Pressure Abnormal
  • Check refrigerant pressure levels.
  • Inspect expansion valve operation.
  • Look for leaks in piping system.
  • Service refrigerant system if needed.
H25
Refrigerant Leakage Detected
  • Inspect copper piping for leaks.
  • Check joints and valves for damage.
  • Repair leak before refilling gas.
  • Perform pressure test after servicing.
H26
Compressor Overheat Protection
  • Allow compressor to cool down.
  • Check condenser fan operation.
  • Clean condenser coils.
  • Inspect compressor load condition.
H27
Compressor Lock Error
  • Check compressor electrical supply.
  • Inspect start capacitor.
  • Measure compressor winding resistance.
  • Replace compressor if seized.
H28
Indoor/Outdoor Miswiring
  • Check wiring connections between units.
  • Verify correct terminal matching.
  • Inspect for loose or reversed wiring.
  • Correct wiring and restart system.
H29
Drain System Blockage
  • Check drain hose for clogging.
  • Clean drainage tray.
  • Ensure proper slope of drain pipe.
  • Remove algae or dirt buildup.
H30
Control PCB Failure
  • Restart system power.
  • Check PCB for burn marks.
  • Inspect connector pins.
  • Replace control board if needed.
H31
Memory / EEPROM Error
  • Reset unit power supply.
  • Check firmware corruption.
  • Replace PCB if error persists.
  • Reinitialize system settings.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner not cooling properly?
Check if the air filter is clean and not blocked. Ensure the temperature is set correctly and windows/doors are closed. Low refrigerant or dirty coils may also reduce cooling performance.
Why does my Panasonic Air Conditioner keep shutting off?
This may be caused by overheating protection, unstable power supply, or a dirty air filter. Turn off the unit, clean the filters, and ensure proper ventilation before restarting.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner not turning on?
Check the power supply, circuit breaker, and remote batteries. Ensure the unit is properly plugged in and the indoor unit panel is securely closed.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner blowing warm air?
Warm air may be caused by incorrect mode settings, low refrigerant, or dirty filters. Set the unit to cooling mode and clean the filters regularly.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner leaking water?
Water leakage is often caused by a clogged drain pipe or dirty filters. Check the drainage system and ensure proper installation and leveling.
Why does my Panasonic Air Conditioner make loud noises?
Noise may come from loose parts, dirty fan blades, or blocked airflow. Inspect the indoor and outdoor units and clean them carefully.
Why does my Panasonic Air Conditioner smell bad?
Bad odors are usually caused by mold, dust, or moisture buildup inside the unit. Clean the filters and consider a deep cleaning of the evaporator coil.
Why is airflow weak from my Panasonic Air Conditioner?
Weak airflow is often due to clogged filters or blocked vents. Clean the filters and ensure nothing is obstructing air circulation.
Why does my Panasonic Air Conditioner keep turning on and off?
This may be caused by thermostat issues, dirty sensors, or incorrect temperature settings. Clean the unit and adjust settings to stabilize operation.
Why is ice forming on my Panasonic Air Conditioner?
Ice buildup can occur due to low refrigerant, poor airflow, or dirty filters. Turn off the unit and allow it to thaw before cleaning and restarting.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner remote not working?
Check and replace the batteries, ensure no obstruction between remote and unit, and confirm the sensor on the indoor unit is clean.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner outdoor unit not running?
This may be due to power issues, capacitor failure, or compressor protection mode. Check the breaker and ensure stable power supply.
Why does my Panasonic Air Conditioner display an error or blinking light?
A blinking light usually indicates a system fault such as sensor issues, communication errors, or protection mode activation. Restart the unit and check filters.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner freezing up?
Freezing may be caused by low airflow, dirty filters, or refrigerant issues. Clean the filters and allow the unit to thaw completely before restarting.
Why is my Panasonic Air Conditioner tripping the breaker?
This may indicate electrical overload, short circuit, or compressor issues. Avoid using extension cords and consult a technician if it continues.
How do I reset my Panasonic Air Conditioner?
Turn off the unit, unplug it or switch off the breaker for 5–10 minutes, then restore power and restart the system.
When should I service my Panasonic Air Conditioner?
Service is recommended if cooling performance drops, unusual noises occur, water leaks persist, or the unit repeatedly shuts down or shows faults.
Maintenance Tip: Regular cleaning of air filters, proper ventilation, and scheduled servicing can help maintain optimal performance and extend the life of your Panasonic Air Conditioner.
