LG Dishwasher Error Codes

Complete LG Dishwasher Troubleshooting Guide

IE
Water Inlet Error
  • Check water supply valve is open.
  • Inspect inlet hose for blockage.
  • Clean inlet filter screen.
  • Ensure proper water pressure.
OE
Drainage Failure
  • Check drain hose for clogs.
  • Clean dishwasher filter.
  • Inspect drain pump operation.
  • Reset the unit.
LE
Motor Lock Error
  • Turn off power for 10 minutes.
  • Check wash motor wiring.
  • Inspect for debris in pump.
  • Restart cycle.
FE
Water Overflow
  • Turn off water supply immediately.
  • Check inlet valve for leakage.
  • Inspect float switch.
  • Dry excess water inside unit.
HE
Heating Error
  • Check heating element wiring.
  • Inspect thermostat sensor.
  • Ensure proper power supply.
  • Replace heater if needed.
AE
Leakage Detected
  • Inspect hoses and seals.
  • Check dishwasher base for water.
  • Dry and restart unit.
  • Contact technician if leak continues.
TE
Temperature Error
  • Inspect temperature sensor.
  • Check wiring harness.
  • Restart dishwasher.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E1
Water Leakage Detected
  • Turn off power and water supply.
  • Check internal hose connections.
  • Inspect base tray for water.
  • Contact service if unresolved.
E3
Drain Pump Issue
  • Clean drain filter.
  • Inspect pump for blockage.
  • Check drain hose installation.
  • Restart dishwasher cycle.
PF
Power Interruption
  • Check household power supply.
  • Reset circuit breaker.
  • Restart wash cycle.
  • Avoid unstable power source.
NE
Diverter Motor Error
  • Inspect diverter motor connection.
  • Check wash arm movement.
  • Clean spray system.
  • Replace motor if needed.
CD
Cool Dry Mode Active
  • This is not an error.
  • Dishwasher is in drying cycle mode.
  • Wait until cycle completes.
  • Cancel cycle if needed.
CL
Child Lock Active
  • Press and hold Child Lock button.
  • Refer to control panel instructions.
  • Wait for lock indicator to disappear.
  • Resume operation.
nE
Vario Motor Error
  • Turn off the dishwasher and disconnect power.
  • Inspect the diverter motor wiring.
  • Check for obstructions in the spray arm system.
  • Restart the dishwasher after a few minutes.
bE
Detergent Usage Error
  • Use only dishwasher-approved detergent.
  • Avoid excessive detergent quantities.
  • Clean excess foam from the tub.
  • Run a rinse cycle to clear residue.
tE1
Thermistor Error
  • Turn off power and restart the dishwasher.
  • Inspect the temperature sensor connection.
  • Check wiring for damage.
  • Replace the thermistor if necessary.
tE2
Water Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Disconnect power for several minutes.
  • Inspect sensor wiring and connectors.
  • Check for corrosion on terminals.
  • Replace the sensor if the error returns.
CE
Current Error
  • Turn off the dishwasher immediately.
  • Check electrical connections.
  • Inspect the wash motor for damage.
  • Contact a technician if the error persists.
PE
Water Level Sensor Error
  • Inspect the water level sensor.
  • Check sensor wiring connections.
  • Clean any debris around the sensor.
  • Replace the sensor if necessary.
F
Excessive Water Fill
  • Turn off the water supply.
  • Inspect the inlet valve for proper operation.
  • Check float switch movement.
  • Restart after correcting the issue.
No Water
Water Supply Problem
  • Verify the water tap is fully open.
  • Inspect the inlet hose for kinks.
  • Check household water pressure.
  • Clean the inlet screen filter.
Door
Door Not Properly Closed
  • Open and firmly close the door.
  • Check the door latch mechanism.
  • Inspect for obstructions around the seal.
  • Restart the wash cycle.
LOC
Control Panel Locked
  • Press and hold the lock button.
  • Wait for the lock indicator to turn off.
  • Refer to the user manual for unlock instructions.
  • Resume normal operation.

Frequently Asked Questions


What does LG dishwasher error code IE mean?

IE indicates a water inlet error. Check the water supply valve, hose, and water pressure.

How do I fix LG dishwasher OE error?

OE means a drain error. Inspect the drain hose, filter, and garbage disposal connection for blockages.

What does LE error mean on an LG dishwasher?

LE indicates a motor or circulation pump issue. Turn off power, restart the dishwasher, and inspect for obstructions.

Can LG dishwasher errors reset automatically?

Some errors may clear after disconnecting power for 5–10 minutes and restarting the dishwasher.

Why does my LG dishwasher keep showing error codes?

Recurring error codes may result from clogged filters, faulty sensors, drainage issues, water supply problems, or electrical faults.

What is the most common LG dishwasher error code?

IE (water inlet error) and OE (drain error) are among the most common LG dishwasher error codes.

Can I continue using my LG dishwasher when an error code appears?

It is not recommended. Continuing operation may cause water leaks, poor cleaning performance, or damage to internal components.

When should I call a technician for LG dishwasher errors?

Contact a technician if the error involves the motor, control board, leak detection system, or if the error returns after troubleshooting.

Important: If the error persists after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified LG appliance technician.
