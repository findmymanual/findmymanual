Toshiba Microwave Error Codes

Complete Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Guide

Door System Errors
E1
Door Switch Fault
  • Ensure microwave door closes properly.
  • Inspect latch mechanism alignment.
  • Check door switch wiring connections.
  • Replace faulty door switch assembly if needed.
Protection System Errors
E2
Overheat Protection
  • Allow microwave to cool completely.
  • Check ventilation openings for blockage.
  • Inspect cooling fan operation.
  • Restart unit after cooling period.
Heating System Errors
E3
Magnetron Failure
  • Check high-voltage components carefully.
  • Inspect magnetron wiring connections.
  • Test capacitor and diode condition.
  • Replace magnetron if defective.
Control System Errors
E4
Control Board Error
  • Disconnect power and restart microwave.
  • Inspect PCB for damage or burn marks.
  • Check internal wiring harness connections.
  • Replace control board if faulty.
Important: Microwave repairs involve high voltage components and should only be performed by a qualified technician.
