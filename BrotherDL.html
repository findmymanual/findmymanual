Brother
❓Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and solutions for GE home appliances and system errors

General Troubleshooting
What does an error code mean on my Brother device?
Error codes indicate issues such as paper jams, toner problems, scanner faults, firmware errors, or hardware communication failures.
Should I restart my Brother printer when it shows an error?
Yes. Restarting can clear temporary faults, but repeated errors usually require deeper troubleshooting.
When should I contact Brother support?
Contact support if errors persist, or if there are issues with the mainboard, scanner unit, or print engine.
Why does my Brother device keep going offline?
This may be caused by network instability, driver issues, or incorrect printer settings.
Printer Issues
Why is my Brother printer not printing?
Common causes include paper jams, empty toner, offline mode, or driver issues.
Why does my Brother printer show “Replace Toner”?
The toner is low or the sensor incorrectly detects depletion. Reset or replacement may be required.
Why is my Brother printer printing blank pages?
Empty toner, clogged drum unit, or incorrect cartridge installation can cause blank prints.
Why is print quality faded or streaky?
Dirty drum unit, low toner, or incorrect print settings may affect print quality.
Why does my printer keep jamming?
Dust, worn rollers, or incorrect paper size can cause frequent jams.
Scanner & All-in-One Issues
Why is my Brother scanner not working?
Driver issues, USB connection problems, or software conflicts may prevent scanning.
Why are scanned images blurry?
Dirty scanner glass, low resolution settings, or movement during scanning can reduce clarity.
Why does my Brother all-in-one show error messages?
This may be caused by firmware issues, paper jams, or toner/drum faults.
Why does scanning stop halfway?
Cable issues, driver crashes, or memory overload may interrupt scanning.
WiFi & Connection Issues
Why won’t my Brother printer connect to WiFi?
Incorrect password, weak signal, or router incompatibility may prevent connection.
Why does my Brother printer keep disconnecting?
Network instability or outdated firmware may cause frequent disconnections.
How do I reset network settings on Brother printers?
Most models allow reset via network settings menu or by holding wireless + cancel buttons.
Hardware & Power Issues
Why does my Brother printer not turn on?
Faulty power cable, blown fuse, or internal power board failure may be the cause.
Why is my Brother device making grinding noise?
Worn gears, paper debris, or roller damage can cause noise during operation.
Can power surges damage Brother printers?
Yes. Surges can damage the mainboard, power supply, and sensors.
Error Code Issues
Why does my Brother printer show “Paper Jam” but no paper is stuck?
Sensor dust or small debris may trigger false jam detection.
Why does my Brother printer show “Toner Error”?
Cartridge not seated properly or chip detection failure may trigger this error.
Why do multiple error lights flash?
This often indicates a general hardware fault or system failure.
Extra Common Questions
Why does my Brother printer smell like burning?
Overheating components or friction inside rollers may cause burning smell.
Why does my printer stop mid-print?
Driver issues, memory limits, or connection dropouts may interrupt printing.
When should I replace instead of repair?
If repair costs are high or the printer is outdated, replacement may be more practical.
Tip: Regular cleaning of rollers and updating drivers helps prevent most Brother printer issues.
