Philips System Messages

Meaning & Quick Troubleshooting Guide

Control & Safety Messages
LOCK
Child Lock Active
  • Press and hold the lock button to disable.
  • Check user manual for unlock procedure.
  • Restart device if controls remain locked.
  • Inspect control panel responsiveness.
HOT
Overheat Warning
  • Turn off device and allow cooling.
  • Check airflow and ventilation openings.
  • Remove any obstructions or dust buildup.
  • Restart after temperature normalizes.
Power & System Messages
PF
Power Failure Detected
  • Check household power supply.
  • Inspect outlet and power cord condition.
  • Reset device after power stabilizes.
  • Verify proper voltage supply.
ERR
General System Error
  • Turn device off and unplug it.
  • Wait several minutes before restarting.
  • Inspect for visible damage or blockage.
  • Contact support if error returns.
RESET
System Restart Required
  • Disconnect power briefly.
  • Reconnect and restart the device.
  • Check if warning message clears.
  • Inspect system controls if issue persists.
COOL
Cooling Cycle Active
  • Allow cooling cycle to complete normally.
  • Do not unplug device during cooling.
  • Ensure proper airflow around the unit.
  • Restart only after cooling finishes.
Important: Repeated system messages may indicate an internal control or sensor issue requiring professional inspection.
