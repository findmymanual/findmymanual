Westinghouse Refrigerator Error Codes

Complete Westinghouse Refrigerator Guide

This guide explains all Westinghouse Refrigerator error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E1
Freezer Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check freezer thermistor connection.
  • Inspect wiring harness for damage or corrosion.
  • Test sensor resistance with multimeter.
  • Replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
E2
Fridge Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Inspect fridge thermistor wiring.
  • Check connector pins for looseness.
  • Test resistance values at room temperature.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E3
Defrost Sensor Failure
  • Inspect defrost sensor wiring for disconnection.
  • Check for ice buildup around sensor area.
  • Test continuity with a multimeter.
  • Replace sensor if readings are abnormal.
E4
Evaporator Fan Fault
  • Check fan blade for ice blockage or obstruction.
  • Inspect wiring connections to fan motor.
  • Test fan operation directly.
  • Replace evaporator fan motor if not working.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to reach proper freezing temperature.
  • Check freezer environment stability.
  • Ensure food items are not blocking airflow.
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes.
E6
Condenser Fan Fault
  • Clean condenser coil and surrounding area.
  • Check fan blade for damage or obstruction.
  • Inspect wiring harness connection.
  • Replace condenser fan motor if needed.
E7
Compressor Overload Protection
  • Allow compressor to cool for 20–30 minutes.
  • Check voltage stability from power supply.
  • Inspect start relay and capacitor.
  • Call technician if compressor keeps tripping.
E8
Defrost Heater Failure
  • Test defrost heater continuity with multimeter.
  • Inspect wiring for burns or looseness.
  • Check defrost cycle operation.
  • Replace heater if defective.
E9
Communication Error (Main Board)
  • Reset power by unplugging refrigerator for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring between control boards.
  • Check connectors for corrosion or looseness.
  • Replace PCB if communication failure continues.
E10
Door Switch Fault
  • Ensure refrigerator door closes properly.
  • Inspect door switch alignment and condition.
  • Check wiring to door sensor.
  • Replace switch if faulty.
E11
High Temperature Alarm
  • Check if door was left open too long.
  • Inspect airflow vents for blockage.
  • Ensure condenser coils are clean.
  • Allow system to stabilize before restart.
E12
Low Voltage / Power Instability
  • Check household voltage supply.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Inspect power plug and outlet condition.
  • Use voltage stabilizer if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Westinghouse refrigerator show system messages or error codes?
System messages usually indicate temperature sensor faults, defrost system issues, fan failures, compressor problems, door switch errors, or control board communication faults. Restart the refrigerator and check the related components if the message returns.
Why does my Westinghouse refrigerator stop cooling suddenly?
Sudden cooling loss may be caused by compressor overload protection, blocked airflow, faulty thermostat, power supply issues, or defrost system failure. Turn off the unit, allow it to stabilize, then restart after checking ventilation and power.
Why is my Westinghouse refrigerator not cooling properly?
Cooling issues may be caused by dirty condenser coils, faulty evaporator fan, refrigerant problems, temperature sensor failure, or incorrect settings. Ensure proper airflow and clean coils regularly.
Why is my Westinghouse refrigerator freezing food in the fresh food compartment?
Over-freezing may be caused by a faulty thermostat, stuck damper, defective temperature sensor, or incorrect temperature settings. Adjust settings and inspect airflow control.
Why is ice building up inside my Westinghouse refrigerator?
Ice buildup is commonly caused by defrost heater failure, defrost sensor issues, door seal leaks, or blocked airflow. Inspect the defrost system and door gasket condition.
Why is my refrigerator not defrosting properly?
Defrost issues may be caused by a faulty defrost timer, heater, or sensor. Check wiring and test defrost components for continuity.
Why is my Westinghouse refrigerator noisy?
Noise may come from evaporator fan, condenser fan, compressor vibration, or ice obstruction. Check for loose components and ice buildup around fans.
Why is the evaporator fan not working?
Fan failure may be caused by motor damage, wiring faults, or ice blockage. Inspect fan blades and test motor operation.
Why is my Westinghouse refrigerator running constantly?
Continuous running may be caused by dirty condenser coils, warm room temperature, faulty sensors, refrigerant issues, or door seal leaks.
Why is my refrigerator compressor not turning on?
Compressor failure may be caused by start relay issues, overload protection, control board faults, or power supply problems. Check electrical components carefully.
Why does my Westinghouse refrigerator door alarm keep beeping?
Door alarms are triggered when the door is open too long, the seal is damaged, or the door switch is faulty. Ensure the door closes properly.
Why is my refrigerator door not sealing properly?
Door seal issues may be caused by damaged gasket, misalignment, or debris on sealing surface. Clean and inspect the gasket regularly.
Why does my Westinghouse refrigerator keep losing power or restarting?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, faulty power outlet, loose wiring, or control board issues. Avoid using extension cords and check power stability.
Why does my refrigerator display a general system error?
General errors usually indicate temporary control board glitches or communication faults. Perform a full power reset to clear the system.
How do I reset Westinghouse refrigerator system messages?
Turn off the refrigerator, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power. This resets the control system and may clear temporary error messages.
When should I service my Westinghouse refrigerator?
Service is recommended if cooling fails, ice buildup persists, compressor issues continue, error codes return after reset, or the unit becomes unusually noisy.
Important: Westinghouse refrigerator system messages are designed to protect key components such as the compressor, evaporator fan, condenser fan, temperature sensors, defrost system, door switches, and control board. If problems continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
