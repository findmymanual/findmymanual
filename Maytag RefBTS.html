Maytag Refrigerator Error Codes

Complete Maytag Refrigerator Guide

This guide explains all Maytag Refrigerator error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

PO
Power Interruption / Outage Detected
  • Check household circuit breaker and power outlet.
  • Unplug refrigerator for 5–10 minutes.
  • Ensure stable voltage supply.
  • Reconfirm temperature settings after restart.
OP
Door Open Alert
  • Ensure refrigerator doors are fully closed.
  • Check door gasket for dirt or damage.
  • Remove items blocking proper sealing.
  • Test door switch response.
CF
Cooling Fan Failure
  • Inspect evaporator fan for ice or obstruction.
  • Check fan motor wiring connections.
  • Listen for fan operation inside freezer.
  • Replace fan motor if not running.
DH
Defrost Heater Fault
  • Test defrost heater continuity.
  • Inspect defrost thermostat connection.
  • Check wiring harness for damage.
  • Replace heater assembly if faulty.
IC
Ice Maker Communication Error
  • Inspect ice maker wiring harness.
  • Check for ice blockage in ice maker assembly.
  • Reset refrigerator power cycle.
  • Replace ice maker module if needed.
IF
Ice Maker Fan Issue
  • Check ice compartment airflow path.
  • Remove frost or ice buildup around fan.
  • Inspect fan motor connection.
  • Replace fan motor if it fails to spin.
CO
Communication Error (Main Control)
  • Unplug unit for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect main control board wiring.
  • Check connectors for corrosion or looseness.
  • Replace control board if communication fails.
EF
Evaporator Fan Circuit Fault
  • Check evaporator fan motor operation.
  • Inspect wiring harness for damage.
  • Ensure no ice blockage in fan area.
  • Replace fan motor or PCB if needed.
LO
Low Temperature Warning
  • Check thermostat settings.
  • Inspect temperature sensor placement.
  • Ensure vents are not blocked by food.
  • Allow unit to stabilize after adjustment.
HI
High Temperature Warning
  • Check if door was left open.
  • Inspect condenser coils for dust buildup.
  • Verify compressor operation.
  • Reduce load inside refrigerator.
SY CE
System Communication Failure
  • Inspect main PCB connections.
  • Check wiring between control boards.
  • Perform full power reset.
  • Replace PCB if issue persists.
SY EF
Evaporator Fan System Error
  • Check evaporator fan motor function.
  • Inspect for frost buildup blocking fan.
  • Verify wiring harness condition.
  • Replace fan or control board if needed.
DR
Drain / Defrost Water Blockage
  • Inspect defrost drain tube for clogging.
  • Clear ice or debris buildup.
  • Ensure proper water flow path.
  • Check heater function if freezing occurs repeatedly.
FC
Freezer Cooling Failure
  • Check compressor operation.
  • Inspect evaporator coils for frost buildup.
  • Verify airflow circulation inside freezer.
  • Call technician if cooling does not recover.
SC
Sensor Communication Error
  • Inspect temperature sensor wiring.
  • Check sensor resistance values.
  • Reset refrigerator power.
  • Replace faulty sensor if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Maytag refrigerator not cooling properly?
Check if the temperature settings are correct. Ensure vents inside the fridge are not blocked by food items. Clean condenser coils and make sure the compressor is running normally. Verify that doors are sealing properly.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator freezing food in the fresh food section?
This may be caused by incorrect temperature settings, blocked air vents, or a faulty damper control. Adjust temperature settings and ensure proper airflow inside the refrigerator compartment.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator making loud noises?
Noises can come from the evaporator fan, condenser fan, or compressor. Check for ice buildup around the fan, ensure the fridge is level, and inspect for loose components or vibrations.
Why is water leaking inside my Maytag refrigerator?
Water leaks are often caused by a clogged defrost drain, cracked water line, or blocked drain tube. Clear the drain line and inspect water connections for leaks or damage.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator ice maker not working?
Check if the ice maker is turned on and the water supply is connected. Inspect the water inlet valve, replace the water filter if clogged, and ensure the freezer temperature is low enough for ice production.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator running constantly?
A refrigerator may run continuously due to dirty condenser coils, frequent door openings, warm ambient temperature, or a failing thermostat. Clean coils and check door seals.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator door not sealing properly?
Inspect the door gasket for dirt, damage, or warping. Clean the seal with warm water and ensure nothing is blocking the door from closing tightly.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator display not working?
This may be caused by a power issue, control board fault, or loose wiring. Check power supply, reset the refrigerator, and inspect control panel connections.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator not dispensing water?
Check if the water line is connected and not frozen or kinked. Replace the water filter if clogged and ensure the dispenser lock is not activated.
Why does my Maytag refrigerator smell bad inside?
Odors are usually caused by spoiled food, dirty drip pans, or clogged drain lines. Clean the interior thoroughly and remove expired food items.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator temperature fluctuating?
Temperature changes can be caused by faulty sensors, blocked vents, or frequent door opening. Ensure proper airflow and check thermostat settings.
Why is frost building up inside my Maytag refrigerator?
Frost buildup may be caused by a faulty defrost system, damaged door seal, or frequent door openings. Inspect the gasket and ensure proper defrost cycle operation.
Why is my Maytag refrigerator not making ice?
Check water supply, ensure freezer temperature is cold enough, and inspect the ice maker assembly. A clogged filter or faulty inlet valve may also be the cause.
How do I reset my Maytag refrigerator?
Unplug the refrigerator or switch off the breaker for 5–10 minutes, then restore power. This can help clear minor electronic glitches and restore normal operation.
How often should I clean my Maytag refrigerator?
Clean the interior every 1–2 months and condenser coils every 6 months. Regular maintenance helps improve cooling efficiency and extends appliance lifespan.
Important: If problems persist after troubleshooting your Maytag refrigerator, disconnect power immediately and stop using the appliance. Continued issues may indicate compressor failure, sealed system problems, or electrical faults. Contact an authorized Maytag technician for proper diagnosis and repair to prevent further damage.
