Haier Television Error Codes

Complete Haier Television Guide

This guide explains all Haier Television error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

001
Network Connection Failure
  • Check Wi-Fi router status.
  • Restart modem and TV.
  • Re-enter Wi-Fi password.
  • Move TV closer to router if signal is weak.
002
Firmware Update Failed
  • Retry update with stable internet.
  • Ensure enough storage space.
  • Restart TV and try again.
  • Use USB update method if needed.
003
Smart Service Unavailable
  • Check internet connection.
  • Verify server status.
  • Restart Smart TV apps.
  • Reset network settings.
004
HDMI Communication Issue
  • Reconnect HDMI cable.
  • Try another HDMI port.
  • Replace damaged cable.
  • Restart external device.
005
Internal Memory Error
  • Restart TV system.
  • Clear app cache and data.
  • Remove unused apps.
  • Perform factory reset if needed.
006
Wi-Fi Module Fault
  • Restart TV system.
  • Check internal Wi-Fi hardware.
  • Update firmware.
  • Replace Wi-Fi module if needed.
007
No Signal Detected
  • Check antenna or HDMI connection.
  • Select correct input source.
  • Restart TV and device.
  • Inspect cable condition.
008
Backlight Failure
  • Check if screen is dark but audio works.
  • Test brightness settings.
  • Inspect backlight inverter circuit.
  • Replace LED backlight strips if needed.
009
Audio Output Failure
  • Check volume and mute settings.
  • Test external speakers/headphones.
  • Inspect audio board circuit.
  • Replace speaker module if faulty.
010
Screen Panel Error
  • Check for lines or flickering display.
  • Inspect LVDS cable connection.
  • Restart TV system.
  • Replace LCD/LED panel if necessary.
011
Remote Control Not Responding
  • Replace remote batteries.
  • Clean IR sensor area.
  • Check for signal blockage.
  • Test remote or replace IR module.
012
System Boot Loop
  • Perform hard reset.
  • Unplug TV for 10 minutes.
  • Update firmware.
  • Factory reset system.
013
Overheating Protection Activated
  • Turn off TV and let it cool.
  • Ensure ventilation is clear.
  • Clean internal dust buildup.
  • Check power board condition.
014
Smart Platform Login Error
  • Check internet connection.
  • Verify account credentials.
  • Restart Smart TV system.
  • Reset Smart TV profile.
015
App Crash / Freezing Issue
  • Restart affected app.
  • Clear app cache.
  • Update Smart TV software.
  • Reinstall application if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Haier television overheating or showing HOT warning?
Make sure the TV has proper ventilation and is not placed in a closed cabinet. Clean dust from air vents, avoid direct sunlight, and turn off the TV to let it cool down before restarting.
Why does my Haier television take time to respond or enter cooling mode?
This usually happens when the TV is processing heavy tasks or protecting internal components. Turn off unnecessary apps, restart the TV, and ensure firmware is up to date.
Why does my Haier television turn off during power fluctuations?
Unstable power supply can cause shutdowns. Check the power outlet, use a surge protector, and avoid plugging the TV into overloaded extension cords.
Why does my Haier television need to be reset frequently?
Frequent resets may indicate software glitches or corrupted settings. Perform a factory reset, update firmware, and avoid installing unstable apps.
Why is my Haier television showing general errors or glitches?
This may be caused by software bugs, weak signal input, or internal conflicts. Restart the TV, check connections, and update system software.
Why does my Haier television freeze or lag?
Too many apps running or low system memory can cause lag. Clear cache, close background apps, and restart the TV.
Why does my Haier television show no signal?
Check HDMI or antenna connection, select the correct input source, and ensure external devices are powered on and working properly.
Why is my Haier television screen dim or dark?
Check brightness settings, power-saving mode, or backlight failure. If sound works but no picture appears, the backlight may need inspection.
Why is there no sound on my Haier television?
Check volume level, mute setting, and audio output source. Try external speakers or reset audio settings if needed.
Why is my Haier television picture distorted or pixelated?
This may be caused by poor signal quality or damaged HDMI cable. Replace the cable, adjust resolution settings, and check signal source.
Why does my Haier television remote not work?
Replace remote batteries, ensure no obstruction between remote and TV sensor, and clean the IR receiver area.
Why does my Haier television restart by itself?
This may be caused by unstable power, overheating, or software issues. Check power supply, update firmware, and reset the TV.
Why is my Haier television slow or freezing on apps?
Clear app cache, uninstall unused apps, and ensure stable internet connection. A system update may also improve performance.
How do I reset my Haier television?
Go to settings and select factory reset, or unplug the TV for 5–10 minutes. This will restore default settings and fix minor system issues.
Important: If the error code continues after troubleshooting, disconnect power immediately and contact a certified GE technician.
