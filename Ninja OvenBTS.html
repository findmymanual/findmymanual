Ninja Oven Error Codes

Complete Ninja Oven Guide

This guide explains all Ninja Oven error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E1
Temperature Sensor Error
  • Turn off and unplug the oven.
  • Allow unit to cool completely.
  • Check sensor wiring connections.
  • Clean sensor area from grease or residue.
  • Replace sensor if issue persists.
E2
Heating Element Issue
  • Disconnect power immediately.
  • Inspect heating element for visible damage.
  • Check wiring and terminal connections.
  • Replace heating element if faulty.
E3
Cooling Fan Malfunction
  • Check fan for dust or obstruction.
  • Ensure fan spins freely.
  • Clean internal airflow channels.
  • Replace fan motor if needed.
E4
Door Sensor Error
  • Ensure oven door is fully closed.
  • Inspect door latch mechanism.
  • Clean sensor contact points.
  • Replace door switch if faulty.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to stabilize at proper operating temperature.
  • Check internal thermal sensor environment.
  • Ensure no grease or blockage near probe.
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes cooling period.
E6
Heating Relay Failure
  • Turn off and unplug unit.
  • Inspect relay connections on control board.
  • Check for burn marks or loose wiring.
  • Replace relay or PCB if needed.
E7
Internal Airflow Circulation Error
  • Inspect internal fan system.
  • Remove grease buildup inside oven.
  • Check airflow channels for blockage.
  • Replace fan if not operating correctly.
E8
Power Regulation Fault
  • Check power outlet stability.
  • Avoid extension cords or overload sockets.
  • Inspect internal power board.
  • Contact technician if issue continues.
HOT
Overheat Warning
  • Turn off oven immediately.
  • Allow full cooling cycle.
  • Check ventilation openings.
  • Remove grease or airflow blockage.
ERR HEAT
Heating System Failure
  • Inspect heating element and wiring.
  • Check control relay operation.
  • Allow unit to cool before restart.
  • Replace heating system if needed.
ERR FAN
Cooling Fan Failure
  • Check fan for blockage or dust.
  • Clean grease buildup around motor.
  • Test fan rotation.
  • Replace fan motor if needed.
ERR9
Control Board Communication Failure
  • Power cycle the oven.
  • Inspect PCB connectors.
  • Check for moisture or corrosion.
  • Replace control board if needed.
ERR10
Overcurrent Protection Triggered
  • Unplug oven immediately.
  • Check for short circuit or overload.
  • Allow cooling before restart.
  • Inspect internal wiring system.
ERR11
Thermal Cutoff Activated
  • Allow oven to cool completely.
  • Check ventilation and airflow paths.
  • Remove internal grease buildup.
  • Restart after cooling period.
ERR12
Sensor Communication Failure
  • Inspect all temperature sensor wiring.
  • Clean internal connectors.
  • Restart system.
  • Replace sensor module if needed.
ERR13
Voltage Instability Detected
  • Use stable power outlet only.
  • Avoid extension cords.
  • Check household voltage fluctuations.
  • Restart after power stabilizes.
ERR14
Moisture Detected Inside Unit
  • Unplug oven immediately.
  • Dry internal components fully.
  • Check for leaks or condensation.
  • Restart only when completely dry.
ERR15
System Initialization Failure
  • Perform full power reset.
  • Check control board boot sequence.
  • Inspect firmware integrity.
  • Replace PCB if necessary.
ERR16
Airflow Sensor Blockage
  • Check airflow channels for obstruction.
  • Clean dust and grease buildup.
  • Inspect internal fan sensors.
  • Reset unit after cleaning.
ERR17
Heating Calibration Error
  • Allow oven to fully cool.
  • Reset calibration system.
  • Check temperature probe accuracy.
  • Replace sensor if unstable.
ERR18
Internal Memory Fault
  • Power reset the oven.
  • Perform factory reset if available.
  • Check firmware corruption.
  • Replace control board if needed.
ERR19
Safety Interlock Failure
  • Ensure door is fully closed.
  • Clean door sensor area.
  • Check locking mechanism alignment.
  • Replace interlock switch if needed.
ERR20
System Sync Failure
  • Power cycle device.
  • Inspect internal module connections.
  • Reset system software.
  • Replace control board if needed.
F21 / E21
Motor / Fan Assembly Failure
  • Check motor/fan for blockage.
  • Inspect mechanical assembly.
  • Remove obstruction or debris.
  • Replace motor if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Ninja Oven not turning on?
Check if the oven is properly plugged into a working outlet. Ensure the door is fully closed and locked, and the rack is correctly installed. Inspect the power cord and try a different outlet. If still unresponsive, the control board or internal fuse may need servicing.
Why does my Ninja Oven suddenly stop during cooking?
This is usually caused by overheating protection, door misalignment, or blocked airflow. Turn off the oven, unplug it, and allow it to cool for 15–20 minutes. Check vents, internal fan, and ensure the door is fully sealed.
Why is my Ninja Oven not cooking food properly?
Incorrect temperature settings, dirty heating elements, or poor airflow can affect cooking performance. Clean the oven interior, check temperature settings, and ensure proper rack placement.
Why does my Ninja Oven smell like burning?
A burning smell may come from grease buildup, food residue, or dust on heating elements. Stop using immediately, unplug the oven, and clean the interior thoroughly before reuse.
Why is my Ninja Oven making loud fan noises?
The fan may be obstructed by grease or debris. Clean internal airflow channels and ensure nothing is blocking the fan blades.
Why is my Ninja Oven cooking unevenly?
Uneven cooking is often caused by blocked airflow, incorrect rack placement, or dirty heating elements. Reposition food and clean the oven interior.
Why does my Ninja Oven leak heat or steam?
Check that the door seal is intact and properly aligned. Clean the gasket area and ensure the door closes tightly during operation.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E1?
E1 indicates temperature sensor error. Inspect sensor wiring, clean surrounding area, and restart the oven after cooling.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E2?
E2 indicates overheating protection activation. Turn off the oven, allow it to cool completely, and check ventilation and airflow.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E3?
E3 refers to heating element failure or instability. Inspect heating components and ensure stable power supply before restarting.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E4?
E4 indicates door sensor or door switch fault. Ensure the door is fully closed and clean the sensor contact area.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E5?
E5 indicates temperature detection fault. Allow the oven to stabilize, check internal sensors, and restart after cooling.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E6?
E6 indicates heating relay failure. Inspect internal relay connections and replace the relay or control board if needed.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E7?
E7 refers to internal airflow circulation error. Clean fan system and remove grease or dust blocking airflow.
Why does my Ninja Oven show E8?
E8 indicates power regulation fault. Check outlet stability, avoid extension cords, and inspect internal power board.
Why does my Ninja Oven show ERR HEAT?
ERR HEAT indicates heating system failure. Inspect heating elements, wiring, and allow cooling before restarting.
Why does my Ninja Oven show ERR FAN?
ERR FAN indicates cooling fan failure. Clean fan blades, remove debris, and test motor operation.
How do I reset my Ninja Oven?
Turn off and unplug the oven for 10–15 minutes. Reconnect power, ensure the door is properly closed, and restart the unit.
Why do Ninja Oven problems keep coming back?
Recurring issues may be caused by grease buildup, faulty sensors, overheating cycles, or internal electrical wear over time.
When should I repair my Ninja Oven?
Seek repair if the oven repeatedly fails to heat, shuts down during use, shows persistent error codes, or has visible electrical or fan issues.
Important: If your Ninja Oven continues to malfunction after troubleshooting, stop using it immediately. Persistent faults may indicate heating element failure, sensor damage, fan system issues, or control board malfunction. Contact an authorized Ninja service center for proper diagnosis and repair.
