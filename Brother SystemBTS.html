Frequently Asked Questions


  • The device is not communicating with your computer or network.
  • Check USB or Wi-Fi connection.
  • Restart the printer or scanner.
  • Set the device as default in system settings.
  • Paper is stuck inside the printer or scanner.
  • Remove jammed paper carefully without tearing it.
  • Check rollers for leftover debris.
  • Reload paper correctly in the tray.
  • The ink or toner level is low or empty.
  • Replace the cartridge with a new one.
  • Reinstall cartridge properly if recently replaced.
  • Reset ink level warning if needed.
  • A printer or scanner cover is not fully closed.
  • Close all covers firmly until they click.
  • Check for obstructions preventing closure.
  • Restart the device after closing covers.
  • The paper tray is empty or not detected.
  • Reload paper correctly in the tray.
  • Adjust paper guides properly.
  • Check sensor for dust or blockage.
  • The device memory is full and cannot process jobs.
  • Cancel pending print or scan jobs.
  • Restart the device to clear temporary memory.
  • Reduce scan resolution or file size.
  • The device cannot connect to the network or computer.
  • Check Wi-Fi or USB cable connection.
  • Restart router and device.
  • Reconfigure network settings if needed.
  • The ink or toner is running low.
  • Prepare a replacement cartridge.
  • Continue printing until quality fades.
  • Replace cartridge when print quality drops.
  • The printer cannot complete the job due to a fault.
  • Restart the printer and clear the queue.
  • Check for paper jams or hardware issues.
  • Update printer drivers if needed.
  • The scanner cannot complete the scan job.
  • Check connection between scanner and computer.
  • Restart scanning software.
  • Ensure drivers are properly installed.
  • Restart the device to clear temporary errors.
  • Check for firmware or driver updates.
  • Inspect hardware for jams or faults.
  • Contact support if messages persist.
Important: If system messages repeat frequently, it may indicate hardware wear or sensor issues requiring inspection.
