Electrolux Dishwasher Error Codes

Complete Electrolux Dishwasher Guide

This guide explains all Electrolux Dishwasher error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

i10
Water Inlet Problem
  • Check that the water supply valve is fully open.
  • Inspect inlet hose for kinks, bends, or blockages.
  • Clean the inlet filter screen to remove debris.
  • Verify household water pressure is sufficient.
i20
Drain Blockage
  • Inspect drain hose for clogs or kinks.
  • Clean dishwasher filter and sump area thoroughly.
  • Check drain pump for debris or obstruction.
  • Ensure sink drain connection is not blocked.
i30
Leak Detected
  • Turn off water supply immediately.
  • Check base pan for water accumulation.
  • Inspect hoses, seals, and connections for leaks.
  • Dry base tray and reset the unit after inspection.
i40
Pressure Sensor Fault
  • Inspect pressure sensor hose for blockage or damage.
  • Check wiring connections for looseness.
  • Clean air trap system carefully.
  • Replace pressure sensor if faulty readings persist.
i50
Wash Motor Failure
  • Check wash pump for blockages or debris.
  • Inspect motor wiring and electrical connections.
  • Test motor resistance for abnormalities.
  • Replace circulation pump if motor fails.
i60
Heating System Issue
  • Check heating element continuity.
  • Inspect thermostat and wiring connections.
  • Verify control board output signal.
  • Replace heating element if defective.
iC0
Control Board Error
  • Reset power for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring harness connections.
  • Check for moisture or corrosion on PCB.
  • Replace main control board if issue persists.
30
Door Open Detected
  • Ensure dishwasher door is fully closed and latched.
  • Check door latch alignment and condition.
  • Inspect door switch sensor for faults.
  • Remove any obstruction preventing closure.
iF0
Water Fill Time Exceeded
  • Check water inlet valve operation.
  • Inspect inlet hose for restrictions.
  • Clean inlet filter screen thoroughly.
  • Ensure adequate household water pressure.
iF1
Low Water Flow Detected
  • Verify water supply is fully open.
  • Check for clogged inlet hose or filter.
  • Inspect water inlet valve for failure.
  • Confirm stable household pressure supply.
iF2
Drain Pump Overrun
  • Check drain hose for improper installation.
  • Inspect pump for obstruction or debris.
  • Clean filter and sump assembly.
  • Reset unit after clearing drainage system.
iD0
Turbidity Sensor Error
  • Clean dishwasher filter and sensor area.
  • Check sensor wiring connections.
  • Run a cleaning cycle with dishwasher cleaner.
  • Replace turbidity sensor if readings fail.
iE0
Water Level Detection Fault
  • Inspect pressure switch and hose.
  • Check for clogs in air trap system.
  • Verify wiring connections to sensor.
  • Replace water level sensor if necessary.
iE1
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check thermistor connection.
  • Inspect wiring for damage or corrosion.
  • Test sensor resistance values.
  • Replace temperature sensor if faulty.
iE2
Heating Relay Failure
  • Inspect control board relay contacts.
  • Check wiring to heating element.
  • Test relay switching operation.
  • Replace control board if relay is defective.
iE3
Motor Communication Error
  • Inspect wiring between motor and PCB.
  • Check for loose connectors.
  • Reset dishwasher control system.
  • Replace motor or board if communication fails.
iE4
Spray Arm Blockage
  • Check spray arms for obstructions.
  • Clean nozzles and remove debris.
  • Ensure proper rotation of arms.
  • Reinstall correctly after cleaning.
iE5
Drain Pump Motor Fault
  • Inspect drain pump for damage.
  • Check electrical wiring connections.
  • Clear debris from pump housing.
  • Replace pump motor if non-functional.
FAQ

  • Ensure door is fully closed and latched.
  • Check power connection.
  • Select a wash program.
  • Disable child lock if active.
FAQ

  • Load dishes correctly.
  • Clean spray arms.
  • Use proper detergent amount.
  • Check water temperature and pressure.
FAQ
Why is my dishwasher not draining?
  • Clean filter and drain area.
  • Check hose for blockage.
  • Inspect sink connection.
  • Reset dishwasher.
FAQ
Why is my dishwasher leaking?
  • Check door seal condition.
  • Ensure unit is level.
  • Inspect hoses for leaks.
  • Avoid overuse of detergent.
FAQ
Why is my dishwasher not filling with water?
  • Check water supply valve.
  • Inspect inlet hose.
  • Clean inlet filter.
  • Verify water pressure.
FAQ

  • Check for loose items inside.
  • Inspect spray arms.
  • Ensure leveling.

FAQ

  • Add rinse aid.

FAQ

  • Run a test cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions


What does i10 Water Inlet Problem mean?
Check that the water supply valve is fully open, inspect inlet hose for blockages, clean inlet filter screen, and ensure proper water pressure.
What does i20 Wash Performance Issue mean?
Check spray arms for blockages, clean dishwasher filter, use proper detergent, and ensure water temperature is adequate.
What does iE4 Spray Arm Blockage mean?
Inspect spray arms for debris, clean nozzles thoroughly, ensure free rotation, and reinstall parts correctly.
What does i30 Leak Detected mean?
Turn off water supply, check hoses and seals for leaks, inspect base tray for water, and dry and reset the unit.
What does i50 Wash Motor Failure mean?
Inspect circulation pump for blockage, check wiring connections, test motor operation, and replace pump if faulty.
What does 30 Door Open Detected mean?
Ensure dishwasher door is fully closed, check latch alignment, inspect door switch, and remove any obstruction.
What does iC0 Control Board Error mean?
Restart power for 5–10 minutes, check wiring connections, inspect for moisture damage, and replace control board if needed.
What does iF2 Drain Pump Overrun mean?
Check drain hose for blockage, inspect pump for debris, clean filter system, and reset dishwasher.
What does iE3 Motor Communication Error mean?
Check wiring between motor and PCB, inspect connectors, restart system, and replace motor or board if needed.
What does iE0 Water Level Fault mean?
Inspect pressure sensor and hose, check for clogs, verify wiring, and replace sensor if faulty.
What does iD0 Turbidity Sensor Error mean?
Clean filter and sensor area, remove buildup, run cleaning cycle, and replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
What does i60 Heating System Issue mean?
Check heating element, inspect thermostat wiring, test control board output, and replace heating component if needed.
What does i40 Pressure Sensor Fault mean?
Inspect pressure hose, check air trap system, verify wiring connections, and replace sensor if necessary.
What does iF0 Water Fill Time Exceeded mean?
Check inlet valve, inspect hose for blockage, clean filter screen, and ensure proper water pressure.
What does iE1 Temperature Sensor Fault mean?
Check thermistor connection, inspect wiring, test resistance values, and replace sensor if faulty.
What does iE5 Drain Pump Motor Fault mean?
Inspect drain pump for damage, check wiring, remove debris, and replace pump motor if necessary.
What does iF1 Low Water Flow mean?
Ensure water supply is open, check inlet hose for blockage, inspect valve operation, and confirm stable pressure.
What does iE2 Heating Relay Failure mean?
Inspect relay contacts on control board, check heating wiring, test relay operation, and replace board if needed.
What does i30 Leak Protection System Active mean?
Check base tray for water, inspect hoses and seals, fix leaks immediately, and reset unit after drying.
What does iC0 System Maintenance Required mean?
Restart dishwasher, clean filters, inspect internal components, and schedule professional maintenance if needed.
Important: Always disconnect power before performing repairs. Contact a qualified technician if the issue persists.
