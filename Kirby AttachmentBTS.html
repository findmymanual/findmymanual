Kirby Attachment System Errors

Accessory & Hose Troubleshooting Guide

Attachment Lock Errors
ATT 1
Attachment Not Locked Properly
  • Remove attachment and reattach firmly.
  • Ensure locking mechanism clicks into place.
  • Check for debris blocking connection point.
  • Test attachment stability before use.
Airflow Compatibility Errors
ATT 2
Accessory Airflow Mismatch Detected
  • Verify correct attachment for model type.
  • Check airflow selector settings.
  • Ensure hose is fully connected.
  • Restart vacuum system after adjustment.
Hose System Errors
ATT 3
Hose Blockage Detected
  • Disconnect hose from unit.
  • Inspect for debris or clogs.
  • Clear blockage using flexible rod or air flow.
  • Reconnect and test suction.
Important: Always ensure attachments are compatible with your Kirby model to prevent airflow and performance issues.
