How to Find Your Camera's Serial & Model Number

Quick overview

Serial Numbers (S/N) uniquely identify your camera, while Model Numbers show the product family. Use these numbers for warranty, support, manuals, and accessories.

Common places to check

1
On the Camera Body

Most cameras have a sticker or metal-etched label on the bottom or side.

2
Inside the Battery Compartment

Some compact cameras hide the serial number inside the battery door.

3
On Lenses (Optional)

Certain kit lenses include serial numbers etched on the lens barrel.

4
Original Packaging

Check the box — barcode labels often show both Model and S/N.

5
Purchase Receipt or Email

Invoices or order confirmation emails may list the serial number.

6
Manufacturer Account / App

Register your camera online to see its serial number (Canon, Nikon, Sony apps or accounts).

Troubleshooting if you can't find a label

  • Remove protective cases or covers.
  • Use a flashlight to inspect small or recessed labels.
  • If the camera is non-functional, check the box, receipt, or manufacturer account.
  • Avoid peeling off internal labels — this may void warranty.

Examples & formats

Model: Canon EOS R6, Nikon Z7 II, Sony A7 IV
Serial: Y6K3A0B12C or 1234567890

🧠 Tip: Camera serial numbers are usually 6–12 characters long and may include letters and numbers (e.g., CAM123456). Models often include brand and series (e.g., Canon EOS R5, Nikon Z6 II).

🔒 Privacy & Safety Note

Serial numbers are unique identifiers. Don’t post them publicly — share privately with authorized support only.

Quick tips

  • Look for small text like SN, S/N, or Serial.
  • Model numbers often include brand and letters/numbers (e.g., EOS R6, Z7 II).
  • Contact the manufacturer with photos and proof of purchase if you can’t locate the serial number.
