Panasonic Refrigerator Error Codes

Complete Panasonic Refrigerator Guide

This guide explains all Panasonic Refrigerator error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

H01
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Check thermistor connection.
  • Inspect wiring for damage or loose terminals.
  • Clean sensor area from frost or debris.
  • Replace sensor if readings are abnormal.
H02
Defrost System Error
  • Check defrost heater operation.
  • Inspect defrost thermostat and timer control.
  • Verify evaporator coil for ice buildup.
  • Replace faulty defrost components if needed.
H03
Evaporator Fan Motor Failure
  • Check fan blades for obstruction or ice blockage.
  • Inspect motor wiring and connector.
  • Clean dust and debris around fan assembly.
  • Replace fan motor if not spinning properly.
H04
Compressor System Fault
  • Check compressor power supply.
  • Inspect start relay and capacitor.
  • Ensure proper ventilation at rear of unit.
  • Call technician if compressor fails to start.
H05
Door Switch Error
  • Ensure refrigerator door closes completely.
  • Check door switch alignment and contact.
  • Clean gasket and magnetic seal area.
  • Replace door switch if faulty.
H06
Control Communication Failure
  • Restart refrigerator power supply.
  • Inspect wiring between main and display board.
  • Check PCB for moisture or corrosion damage.
  • Replace control board if communication fails.
H07
Condenser Fan Motor Fault
  • Check condenser fan for dust or blockage.
  • Inspect fan motor wiring connection.
  • Ensure fan rotates freely.
  • Replace fan motor if necessary.
H08
Overload Protection Activated
  • Unplug unit and allow it to rest for 10–15 minutes.
  • Check for blocked airflow or dirty coils.
  • Reduce food load inside refrigerator.
  • Restart after cooling period.
H09
Low Voltage / Power Supply Issue
  • Check power outlet stability.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Inspect wiring and plug condition.
  • Reset breaker if necessary.
H10
Ice Maker System Fault
  • Check water supply line for blockage.
  • Inspect ice maker motor and sensor.
  • Ensure freezer temperature is correct.
  • Reset ice maker module if needed.
H11
Refrigerant Flow Abnormality
  • Check for refrigerant leaks.
  • Inspect expansion valve operation.
  • Verify cooling performance of evaporator.
  • Call technician for refrigerant servicing.
H12
Defrost Heater Malfunction
  • Inspect defrost heater continuity.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Verify control board output signal.
  • Replace heater if no continuity detected.
H13
Temperature Imbalance Detected
  • Check airflow inside refrigerator compartments.
  • Ensure vents are not blocked by food items.
  • Clean evaporator fan system.
  • Reset unit and monitor temperature stability.
H14
Main Control Board Fault
  • Restart refrigerator system.
  • Inspect PCB for burn marks or moisture damage.
  • Check wiring harness connections.
  • Replace main board if error persists.
H15
Sensor Communication Error
  • Check all sensor wiring connections.
  • Inspect for loose or corroded terminals.
  • Reset system power supply.
  • Replace sensor or PCB if needed.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to stabilize to normal operating temperature.
  • Check freezer environment stability and airflow.
  • Ensure food items are not blocking sensors.
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator not cooling properly?
Make sure the temperature setting is correctly adjusted and not accidentally set too warm. Check that the door seals tightly and is not left open for long periods. Clean the condenser coils and ensure there is proper airflow inside the refrigerator. Overloading can also affect cooling performance.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator not turning on?
Check the power outlet, circuit breaker, and plug connection. Ensure the refrigerator is properly connected to a stable power source. If there is still no power, the internal fuse or control board may need inspection.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator making loud noises?
Noise may come from the compressor, evaporator fan, or loose components. Ensure the refrigerator is level and not touching walls or cabinets. Clean the fan area and check for ice buildup that may block movement.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator leaking water?
Water leakage is often caused by a clogged defrost drain or improper leveling. Check the drainage system and clean any blockages. Also ensure door seals are intact to prevent excess condensation.
Why does my Panasonic Refrigerator smell bad?
Bad odor is usually caused by spoiled food, dirty shelves, or mold buildup. Remove expired items, clean all compartments, and check the drainage tray for standing water.
Why is frost building up inside my Panasonic Refrigerator?
Frost buildup can occur due to frequent door opening, damaged door seals, or a faulty defrost system. Check the gasket, avoid leaving the door open, and ensure proper airflow.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator not freezing properly?
Check freezer temperature settings and ensure vents are not blocked. Low refrigerant levels or a faulty compressor may also affect freezing performance.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator door not closing properly?
Inspect door gaskets for dirt, damage, or misalignment. Clean the seals and ensure nothing inside the fridge is blocking the door from closing fully.
Why does my Panasonic Refrigerator run continuously?
Continuous running may be caused by warm ambient temperature, dirty condenser coils, or faulty thermostat settings. Clean coils and adjust temperature settings properly.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator light not working?
Check the bulb or LED module and ensure the door switch is functioning. Replace the light component if necessary.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator freezing food in the fridge compartment?
This may be caused by incorrect temperature settings or blocked air vents. Adjust settings and ensure proper airflow inside the refrigerator.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator vibrating or shaking?
Vibration usually happens when the refrigerator is not level or the compressor is running unevenly. Adjust the feet and ensure it is placed on a stable surface.
Why is my Panasonic Refrigerator door sweating or forming condensation?
Condensation is caused by humidity, frequent door opening, or damaged seals. Clean the gasket and reduce how often the door is opened.
How do I reset my Panasonic Refrigerator?
Unplug the refrigerator for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power. This allows the system to reset and stabilize.
When should I service my Panasonic Refrigerator?
Service is recommended if cooling is weak, unusual noises occur, water leaks persist, or the refrigerator stops maintaining proper temperature.
Important: If your Panasonic Refrigerator continues to malfunction after troubleshooting, stop using it immediately. Persistent issues may indicate compressor failure, refrigerant leaks, thermostat faults, or control board problems. Contact an authorized Panasonic service center for proper diagnosis and repair.
