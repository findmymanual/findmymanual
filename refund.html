Refund Policy

Our Commitment

At Find My Manual, all digital manuals are delivered electronically after your one-time payment of $14.95 per manual. We are committed to providing a safe, secure, and hassle-free experience for all customers.

Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of your purchase for any reason. This refund period is unconditional and complies with Paddle’s refund requirements for digital products.

Refund Process

To request a refund, email us at ✉️findmymanual@outlook.com including your order number and brand/model of the requested manual. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after approval. Some banks may take additional time to reflect the refunded amount.

Partial Refunds

In rare cases, partial refunds may be issued. This can occur if a manual has been partially delivered or if additional services were requested during the order. Any partial refund amount will be clearly communicated and approved before processing.

Non-Refundable Situations

➤Requests made after 7 days of purchase.
➤Manuals that are successfully delivered and accessible to the customer.
➤Errors caused by providing incorrect device or serial information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long will it take to receive my refund?

A: Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days after approval. Some banks may take a few additional days to reflect the amount in your account.

A: If incorrect information prevented us from delivering the manual, please submit a corrected request. If we are unable to locate the correct manual, we will also reach out to you to confirm the correct serial number and model. Refund eligibility will be reviewed accordingly.

Q: Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

A: Yes, as long as we haven’t delivered the manual yet. Since this is a one-time digital product, refunds are only available before the manual is sent.
