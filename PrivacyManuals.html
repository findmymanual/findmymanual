Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how UserManual Store collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and services.

When you use our website, we may collect personal information including your name, email address, and product brand/model details to deliver requested manuals and provide support. We use collected information to deliver purchased manuals, process secure transactions, respond to customer inquiries, improve website functionality, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill orders, provide support, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. These partners are required to safeguard your information.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including HTTPS encryption and secure hosting to protect your data from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

You may adjust your browser settings to control cookies or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, technology, or our business practices.
