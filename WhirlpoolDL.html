Whirlpool FAQ - Troubleshooting & Error Codes

Common questions and solutions for Whirlpool home appliances and system issues

General Troubleshooting (Whirlpool Appliances)
What does an error code mean on Whirlpool appliances?
Error codes indicate problems detected in components such as motors, sensors, water supply, drainage, heating elements, or control boards.
Should I reset my Whirlpool appliance when it shows an error?
Yes. Unplugging the appliance for 1–5 minutes can clear temporary system errors. If the error returns, further inspection is needed.
When should I call a Whirlpool technician?
Call a technician if there are repeated error codes, burning smells, water leaks, electrical issues, or complete failure to operate.
Whirlpool Washing Machine Issues
Why is my Whirlpool washing machine not spinning?
This may be caused by unbalanced load, lid lock issues, motor problems, or drainage blockage.
What does F21 or SD error mean?
F21 usually indicates a drainage issue, while SD refers to excessive suds from too much detergent.
Why is my Whirlpool washer not draining?
Clogged drain hose, blocked pump filter, or faulty drain pump are common causes.
Why is my washing machine shaking violently?
Uneven load distribution or unstable floor surface can cause heavy vibration.
Why is my Whirlpool washer not filling with water?
This may be due to blocked inlet valves, low water pressure, or faulty water level sensor.
Whirlpool Dryer Issues
Why is my Whirlpool dryer not heating?
Common causes include blown thermal fuse, faulty heating element, or thermostat failure.
Why does my dryer take too long to dry clothes?
Clogged lint filter, blocked vent, or overheating sensor issues can reduce drying efficiency.
Why does my dryer stop mid-cycle?
Overheating protection or electrical interruptions may cause mid-cycle shutdowns.
Whirlpool Refrigerator Issues
Why is my Whirlpool refrigerator not cooling?
Possible causes include dirty condenser coils, faulty evaporator fan, or thermostat issues.
Why is my fridge leaking water?
Blocked defrost drain or damaged door seals can cause water leakage.
Why is my Whirlpool fridge making loud noise?
Noises may come from compressor vibration, fan motor issues, or ice buildup.
Why is my freezer not freezing properly?
Airflow blockage, low refrigerant, or faulty defrost system may cause freezing issues.
Whirlpool Dishwasher Issues
Why is my Whirlpool dishwasher not cleaning properly?
Clogged spray arms, low water pressure, or detergent issues may reduce cleaning performance.
Why is my dishwasher not draining?
Blocked drain hose or pump filter is usually the cause.
What does F2E2 error mean?
This indicates communication failure between control board and user interface.
Power & Sensor Issues
Why does my Whirlpool appliance keep restarting?
Power instability or faulty control boards can cause repeated restarts.
Can sensors cause Whirlpool error codes?
Yes. Temperature, water level, and door sensors can trigger system errors when faulty.
Why does my Whirlpool appliance stop mid-cycle?
This may be caused by overheating, power interruptions, or internal electronic faults.
Tip: Whirlpool appliances often show error codes related to drainage, heating, or motor systems—most issues can be diagnosed with basic checks first.
