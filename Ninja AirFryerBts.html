Ninja Air Fryer Error Codes Troubleshooting Guide

Complete Repair & Diagnostic Instructions

Air Fryer Error Codes

E1
Temperature Sensor Error
  • Turn OFF and unplug the unit.
  • Let it cool for 10–15 minutes.
  • Check sensor connection inside heating chamber.
  • Replace temperature sensor if faulty.
E2
Unit Overheating
  • Stop operation immediately.
  • Ensure proper ventilation around unit.
  • Clean air vents and fan area.
  • Allow cooling before restarting.
E3
Heating Element Malfunction
  • Inspect heating coil for damage.
  • Check electrical continuity.
  • Reset power supply.
  • Replace heating element if needed.
E4
Internal Circuit Issue
  • Turn OFF power immediately.
  • Inspect wiring inside control board area.
  • Look for burnt components.
  • Replace control board if required.
E5
Fan Motor Error
  • Check fan blade obstruction.
  • Clean dust buildup.
  • Test fan motor operation.
  • Replace motor if faulty.
E6
Power Supply Problem
  • Check wall outlet voltage.
  • Try different power socket.
  • Inspect power cord damage.
  • Replace power supply board if needed.
E7
Control Board Communication Error
  • Reset the unit by unplugging for 10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring between PCB components.
  • Check for moisture damage.
  • Replace control board if necessary.
E8
Sensor Calibration Failure
  • Reset device to factory settings.
  • Clean internal sensors.
  • Check wiring connections.
  • Replace sensor if calibration fails.
E9
Power Fluctuation Detected
  • Use stable power source.
  • Avoid extension cords.
  • Check household voltage stability.
  • Restart device after stabilization.
E10
Internal Overheating Warning
  • Stop cooking immediately.
  • Allow unit to cool completely.
  • Clean vents and airflow channels.
  • Do not overload cooking basket.
E11
Airflow Blockage Detected
  • Check air intake and exhaust vents.
  • Remove grease or dust buildup.
  • Ensure proper spacing around unit.
  • Restart after cleaning.
E12
Control Board Reset Required
  • Unplug unit for 10–15 minutes.
  • Press reset (if available).
  • Check firmware or internal settings.
  • Replace PCB if issue persists.
SHUT OFF
Overheat Protection Activated
  • Turn OFF and unplug immediately.
  • Let unit cool for at least 30 minutes.
  • Clean vents and internal fan area.
  • Do not restart until fully cooled.
Important: If error continues after troubleshooting, contact Ninja customer support or a qualified appliance technician.
