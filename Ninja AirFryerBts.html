Ninja Air Fryer Error Codes

Complete Ninja Air Fryer Guide

This guide explains all Ninja Air Fryer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E1
Temperature Sensor Error
  • Turn OFF and unplug the air fryer.
  • Allow unit to cool for 10–15 minutes.
  • Check internal temperature sensor connection.
  • Replace sensor if readings are incorrect.
E2
Overheating Protection Activated
  • Stop operation immediately.
  • Clear airflow vents and surrounding space.
  • Allow full cooling before restart.
  • Avoid overloading the basket.
E3
Heating Element Malfunction
  • Check heating coil for visible damage.
  • Inspect electrical connections.
  • Restart unit after reset.
  • Replace heating element if necessary.
E4
Internal Circuit / PCB Error
  • Unplug unit immediately.
  • Check for moisture or burnt components.
  • Reset power after 10 minutes.
  • Replace control board if fault persists.
E5
Fan Motor Failure
  • Check fan blade for obstruction.
  • Clean dust and grease buildup.
  • Test fan rotation manually (power off).
  • Replace fan motor if not spinning.
E6
Power Supply Issue
  • Try a different wall outlet.
  • Avoid extension cords.
  • Inspect power cable for damage.
  • Replace power board if needed.
E7
Control Communication Error
  • Unplug unit for 10 minutes.
  • Check wiring between PCB and display.
  • Restart system.
  • Replace control board if recurring.
E8
Sensor Calibration Failure
  • Reset unit to factory settings.
  • Clean internal sensors carefully.
  • Check wiring harness.
  • Replace sensor if calibration fails.
E9
Voltage Fluctuation Detected
  • Use stable power source.
  • Avoid shared high-load sockets.
  • Check household voltage stability.
  • Restart after power stabilizes.
E10
Airflow Blockage
  • Clean air intake and exhaust vents.
  • Remove grease or dust buildup.
  • Ensure 10–15 cm clearance around unit.
  • Restart after cleaning.
E11
Basket Detection Error
  • Reinsert cooking basket properly.
  • Check safety switch alignment.
  • Clean basket sensor area.
  • Test without load.
E12
Control Reset Required
  • Unplug unit for 10–15 minutes.
  • Press reset button if available.
  • Restore default settings.
  • Replace PCB if issue continues.
E13
Timer System Error
  • Reset cooking timer.
  • Power cycle the unit.
  • Check control panel response.
  • Replace display module if needed.
E14
Display / Panel Error
  • Clean touch panel surface.
  • Check for moisture or grease.
  • Restart unit.
  • Replace control panel if unresponsive.
E15
Moisture or Steam Sensor Alert
  • Turn off and dry internal chamber.
  • Avoid excess liquid inside basket.
  • Clean sensor area.
  • Restart after full drying.
E16
Smoke or Burn Detection
  • Stop cooking immediately.
  • Check for burnt food or oil residue.
  • Clean heating chamber thoroughly.
  • Ensure proper cooking temperature settings.
E17
Heating Coil Efficiency Error
  • Inspect heating element performance.
  • Check for uneven heating.
  • Reset unit and retest.
  • Replace coil if weak or damaged.
E18
Thermal Fuse Triggered
  • Unplug and allow full cooling.
  • Check internal temperature safety fuse.
  • Inspect for overheating cause.
  • Replace fuse if blown.
E19
Touch Panel Malfunction
  • Clean control surface.
  • Remove moisture or oil buildup.
  • Restart system.
  • Replace touch panel if unresponsive.
E20
System Firmware Error
  • Power cycle the unit.
  • Reset system settings.
  • Check for firmware update (if supported).
  • Replace control board if persistent.
SHUT OFF
Emergency Overheat Shutdown
  • Turn OFF and unplug immediately.
  • Allow at least 30 minutes cooling time.
  • Clean vents and internal fan area.
  • Do not restart until fully cooled.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Ninja Air Fryer not turning on?
Check the power outlet and ensure the plug is securely connected. Inspect the power cord for damage. Try a different socket and reset the unit by unplugging it for 5–10 minutes before restarting.
Why is my Ninja Air Fryer not heating?
Ensure the correct cooking mode and temperature are selected. Check for overheating protection activation. Clean internal heating elements and vents, then restart the unit.
Why is my Ninja Air Fryer smoking during use?
Smoke is usually caused by excess oil or food residue inside the basket. Clean the cooking chamber thoroughly and avoid overfilling or using excessive oil.
Why is my Ninja Air Fryer not cooking food evenly?
Uneven cooking may result from overcrowding the basket or improper food placement. Shake or rotate food halfway through cooking for better results.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer stop mid-cycle?
This may be caused by overheating protection, power interruptions, or a loose basket connection. Allow the unit to cool and ensure the basket is properly inserted.
Why is my Ninja Air Fryer making loud fan noises?
Loud fan noise may come from food debris, loose components, or blocked airflow. Clean the fan area and ensure nothing is obstructing internal airflow.
Why is my Ninja Air Fryer displaying a SHUT OFF message?
The unit has activated overheat protection. Turn it off immediately, unplug it, and allow it to cool completely before restarting.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E1 error?
An E1 error typically indicates a temperature sensor issue. Turn off the unit, let it cool, and check sensor connections. Service may be required if it persists.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E2 overheating error?
E2 means the unit is overheating. Stop using it immediately, clean air vents, ensure proper ventilation, and allow the unit to cool before restarting.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E3 heating error?
This indicates a heating element malfunction. Inspect for blockages, reset the unit, and contact service if the heating system does not recover.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E4 internal error?
E4 suggests a control board or internal circuit issue. Power cycle the unit and inspect for moisture or electrical faults. Technician service may be required.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E5 fan error?
E5 indicates a fan malfunction or blockage. Clean the fan area, check for debris, and ensure the fan spins freely before restarting.
Why does my Ninja Air Fryer show an E6 power error?
E6 points to unstable power supply or voltage issues. Try a different outlet and avoid using extension cords or overloaded circuits.
Why do Ninja Air Fryer problems keep coming back?
Recurring issues may indicate overheating, faulty sensors, airflow blockage, or control board failure that requires professional inspection.
When should I call a technician for my Ninja Air Fryer?
Contact a technician if the unit repeatedly shuts off, shows persistent error codes, fails to heat, or emits unusual burning smells after cleaning.
Important: If your Ninja Air Fryer continues to malfunction after troubleshooting, disconnect it from power immediately. Persistent faults may indicate heating element failure, sensor damage, airflow obstruction, or control board issues. Continued use may cause further damage or safety risks. Have the unit inspected by an authorized service technician.
