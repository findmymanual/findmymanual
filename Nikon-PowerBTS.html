Nikon Power / Battery Error Codes

Complete Nikon Power / Battery Guide

This guide explains all Nikon Power / Battery error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

BATTERY EMPTY
Battery Fully Discharged
  • Recharge or replace battery immediately.
  • Check battery contacts for dirt or corrosion.
  • Use original Nikon battery if possible.
  • Verify charger is working properly.
LOW BATTERY
Battery Level Critical
  • Stop shooting and recharge battery.
  • Turn off power-heavy features (LCD, Wi-Fi, GPS).
  • Carry a fully charged spare battery.
  • Avoid frequent deep discharge cycles.
BATTERY WARN
Battery Performance Warning
  • Check if battery is aging or swollen.
  • Test with a different Nikon-compatible battery.
  • Clean battery terminals carefully.
  • Replace battery if runtime is significantly reduced.
CHARGE
Battery Charging Required
  • Connect battery to compatible Nikon charger.
  • Ensure correct battery alignment in charger slot.
  • Check charger indicator light status.
  • Replace battery if it fails to hold charge.
CHARGE ERR
Charging System Error
  • Try a different charger or USB power source.
  • Inspect charger cable and adapter.
  • Clean battery charging contacts.
  • Test with another compatible battery.
POWER ERROR
Power System Fault
  • Remove and reinsert battery securely.
  • Check battery compatibility with Nikon model.
  • Inspect camera battery compartment contacts.
  • Reset camera or update firmware.
POWER FAIL
Unexpected Power Shutdown
  • Replace battery with a fully charged one.
  • Avoid using unstable or third-party batteries.
  • Check for overheating conditions.
  • Inspect internal power circuit if recurring.
BATTERY LOCK
Battery Not Detected / Locked
  • Remove and reinstall battery properly.
  • Ensure battery latch is fully engaged.
  • Clean battery contacts gently.
  • Try a different battery if issue persists.
ERR POWER
Internal Power Circuit Error
  • Power cycle the camera (remove battery for 5–10 min).
  • Check for firmware updates.
  • Inspect battery compartment for damage.
  • Service camera if error persists.
VOLT ERR
Voltage Instability Detected
  • Use only recommended Nikon battery types.
  • Avoid low-quality third-party batteries.
  • Check for overheating battery conditions.
  • Replace battery if voltage is unstable.
AUTO OFF
Auto Power Shutdown Activated
  • Check auto-off timer settings.
  • Replace low or unstable battery.
  • Avoid prolonged idle operation.
  • Disable power-saving mode if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why is my Nikon camera not turning on?
Check if the battery is properly inserted and fully charged. Clean the battery contacts carefully. Try a different compatible Nikon battery. If the issue continues, inspect the power circuit or battery compartment for damage.
Why does my Nikon camera suddenly turn off?
This is usually caused by a weak or aging battery, unstable power connection, or overheating. Replace the battery, avoid extreme temperatures, and ensure proper power contact inside the battery chamber.
Why is my Nikon battery draining so fast?
Fast battery drain may be caused by continuous LCD use, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth activation, cold temperatures, or an old battery. Turn off unused features and test with a fresh battery.
Why does my Nikon camera show a low battery warning too quickly?
The battery may be degraded or not fully compatible. Charge the battery completely, clean the contacts, and test with an original Nikon battery if possible.
Why is my Nikon battery not charging?
Check the charger, cable, and power outlet. Ensure the battery is correctly seated in the charger. Try another charger or battery to isolate the issue.
Why does my Nikon charger light blink or show an error?
A blinking charger light usually indicates a faulty battery, poor contact, or incompatible charger. Clean battery terminals and test with a different battery.
Why is my Nikon battery not detected?
Remove and reinsert the battery properly. Check for dirt or corrosion on contacts. Ensure the battery is Nikon-compatible and not physically damaged.
Why does my Nikon camera shut down during shooting?
This may happen due to power instability, overheating battery, or poor contact. Replace the battery and ensure it is securely locked in place.
Why does my Nikon battery charge but not last long?
The battery is likely worn out or aging. Recharge cycles reduce capacity over time. Replace with a new original Nikon battery for best performance.
Why does my Nikon camera show power error messages?
Power errors may indicate battery incompatibility, dirty contacts, or internal power circuit issues. Restart the camera and test with a different battery.
Why is my Nikon battery overheating?
Overheating may occur during heavy shooting, charging issues, or defective batteries. Stop using the battery immediately and allow it to cool before testing again.
How do I reset Nikon power and battery issues?
Turn off the camera, remove the battery for 5–10 minutes, then reinsert it. This helps reset temporary power system faults.
Why do Nikon power problems keep coming back?
Recurring issues may indicate a failing battery, damaged battery contacts, or internal power board problems that require servicing.
When should I replace my Nikon battery?
Replace the battery if it drains unusually fast, fails to charge, overheats, or no longer holds a charge for normal shooting duration.
When should I bring my Nikon camera for repair?
Seek professional service if the camera does not power on even with a new battery, shuts down repeatedly, or shows persistent power-related errors.
Important: If Nikon power or battery issues continue after troubleshooting, stop using the camera. Persistent faults may indicate internal power circuit failure, damaged battery terminals, or motherboard issues. Continued use may worsen the damage. Contact an authorized Nikon service center for proper diagnosis and repair.
