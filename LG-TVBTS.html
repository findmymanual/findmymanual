LG Television Error Codes

Complete LG Television Guide

This guide explains all LG Television error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

No Signal
LG TV Input Source Issue
  • Ensure the correct input source (HDMI/AV) is selected.
  • Check HDMI or AV cable connection for looseness.
  • Try switching to another HDMI port.
  • Restart the TV and connected device.
Boot Loop
LG TV Software Restart Loop
  • Unplug the TV for 5–10 minutes.
  • Perform a soft reset of the system.
  • Disconnect all external devices.
  • Update firmware if the TV loads successfully.
Black Screen
LG TV Display Failure
  • Check power cable and outlet connection.
  • Increase brightness and picture settings.
  • Use flashlight test to check faint image.
  • Contact technician if no display appears.
WiFi Error
LG TV Network Connection Issue
  • Restart router and TV.
  • Re-enter WiFi password carefully.
  • Check network settings and signal strength.
  • Move TV closer to router if possible.
HDMI No Signal
LG TV HDMI Connection Failure
  • Replace or reconnect HDMI cable.
  • Try a different HDMI port.
  • Ensure external device is powered on.
  • Restart both TV and device.
Firmware Update Loop
LG TV Software Update Issue
  • Disconnect internet connection.
  • Restart the TV.
  • Attempt manual firmware update.
  • Perform factory reset if issue continues.
Audio Error
LG TV Sound System Issue
  • Check volume and mute settings.
  • Test external speakers or soundbar.
  • Restart TV system.
  • Reset audio settings to default.
Remote Not Working
LG TV Remote Sensor Issue
  • Replace remote batteries.
  • Check IR sensor line of sight.
  • Re-pair the remote with the TV.
  • Restart TV if unresponsive.
App Not Available
LG Smart TV App Issue
  • Check internet connection stability.
  • Update LG webOS software.
  • Reinstall the affected app.
  • Restart the TV.
Network Disconnected
LG TV Internet Connection Lost
  • Restart router and TV.
  • Reconnect to WiFi network.
  • Check ISP or internet status.
  • Reset network settings if needed.
Update Failed
LG TV Firmware Update Failure
  • Ensure stable internet connection.
  • Retry update after restart.
  • Use USB update method if available.
  • Factory reset if update keeps failing.
Power ON Failure
LG TV No Power Issue
  • Check power cable and outlet connection.
  • Try a different wall socket.
  • Unplug TV for 10 minutes then retry.
  • Contact technician if TV remains off.
No Picture
LG TV Display Backlight Issue
  • Increase brightness settings.
  • Check input source selection.
  • Perform flashlight test for faint image.
  • Replace backlight or T-Con board if needed.
Vertical Lines
LG TV Panel Display Issue
  • Restart TV and check display.
  • Try different HDMI input/cable.
  • Reset TV to factory settings.
  • Contact technician for panel inspection.
Frozen Screen
LG TV System Hang Issue
  • Unplug TV for 5–10 minutes.
  • Disconnect all external devices.
  • Perform soft reset.
  • Update firmware if accessible.
Apps Crashing
LG TV Software/App Stability Issue
  • Clear app cache and data.
  • Update LG webOS system.
  • Reinstall affected apps.
  • Reset Smart TV settings if needed.
Sound but No Picture
LG TV Backlight Failure
  • Increase brightness to maximum.
  • Check screen using flashlight test.
  • Restart TV system.
  • Replace backlight strips if necessary.
Bluetooth Not Connecting
LG TV Wireless Module Issue
  • Turn Bluetooth off and on again.
  • Restart TV and device.
  • Remove and re-pair devices.
  • Update TV firmware.
Screen Flickering
LG TV Display Stability Issue
  • Replace or check HDMI cable.
  • Lower refresh rate settings.
  • Restart TV system.
  • Inspect power board if issue continues.
Overheating Warning
LG TV Ventilation Issue
  • Turn off TV and allow it to cool.
  • Ensure proper airflow around unit.
  • Clean dust from ventilation areas.
  • Avoid blocking rear panel.
No Sound
LG TV Audio Output Failure
  • Check mute and volume settings.
  • Switch audio output mode.
  • Test external speakers or soundbar.
  • Reset sound settings to default.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my LG TV show “No Signal”?
This usually happens when the input source is incorrect or the HDMI cable is loose. Check the selected input, reconnect HDMI cables, and ensure the external device is powered on. Try switching to another HDMI port if the issue continues.
What does LG TV error code 137 mean?
Error 137 is commonly related to network or app connectivity issues. Restart your router, reconnect Wi-Fi, and reboot the TV. If the error continues, reset network settings and try again.
What does LG TV error code 106 mean?
Error 106 indicates that the TV cannot properly connect to the internet. Check your Wi-Fi password, ensure stable internet access, and restart both the router and TV.
Why is my LG TV not connecting to Wi-Fi?
This may be caused by router issues, incorrect network credentials, or software glitches. Restart the TV and router, forget the network, and reconnect with the correct password.
What does LG TV error code 202 mean?
Error 202 usually indicates a DNS or internet connection failure. Try restarting your network, changing DNS settings to automatic or Google DNS, and reconnecting the TV.
Why does my LG TV keep buffering on apps?
Buffering is usually caused by slow or unstable internet connection. Use a stronger Wi-Fi signal, restart your router, or switch to a wired Ethernet connection for better stability.
Why is there sound but no picture on my LG TV?
This can be caused by backlight failure or display settings issues. Increase brightness, test with a flashlight, restart the TV, and check HDMI connections for faults.
Why does my LG TV keep turning off by itself?
This may be due to sleep timer settings, power saving mode, overheating, or unstable power supply. Disable timers, ensure proper ventilation, and check power settings.
What does a blinking red light on LG TV mean?
A blinking red light usually indicates a power supply or internal hardware issue. Unplug the TV for a few minutes, then restart. If it continues, inspect the power board.
Why is my LG TV remote not working?
This may be caused by low batteries, pairing issues, or sensor obstruction. Replace the batteries, re-pair the remote, and ensure nothing is blocking the IR sensor.
How do I fix LG TV apps not opening?
Apps may not open due to outdated software or corrupted cache. Update your TV firmware, clear app cache, or reinstall the affected apps to resolve the issue.
What does LG TV error “Unable to connect to server” mean?
This indicates a temporary internet or server issue. Check your connection, restart your router, and try again later if servers are down.
Why is my LG TV screen flickering?
Screen flickering can be caused by loose HDMI cables, incorrect refresh rate settings, or hardware issues. Check all connections and adjust display settings.
How do I reset my LG TV?
Go to Settings > General > Reset to Initial Settings. This will restore factory defaults and may resolve software-related issues.
What should I do if LG TV error codes keep appearing?
If error codes continue after troubleshooting, update the firmware, reset the TV, or contact LG support for hardware inspection.
Important notice for LG TV issues
If the problem persists after troubleshooting, disconnect the TV from power and contact a qualified LG technician or LG support for further assistance. Avoid repeated use if hardware failure is suspected.
Important: If the issue persists after troubleshooting, disconnect the LG TV from the power source and allow it to rest for a few minutes before attempting another restart. Continued operation while recurring errors, display issues, sound failure, or system instability are present may lead to further damage to internal components such as the power board, main board, backlight system, or software firmware. If the problem continues after a reset or power cycle, do not repeatedly force restart the television, as this may worsen the condition. Instead, stop using the device and ensure all external devices such as HDMI cables, gaming consoles, or streaming devices are disconnected before further testing. For safety and proper diagnosis, contact an authorized LG Television technician or LG support center for a full inspection. Professional servicing is recommended especially when the issue involves power failure, no display, repeated boot loops, overheating, or persistent error codes, as these may require internal component replacement or advanced repair procedures.
