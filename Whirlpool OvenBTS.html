Whirlpool Oven / Range Error Codes

Complete Oven / Range Dryer Guide

This guide explains all Oven / Range Dryer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

F0
Electronic Control Failure
  • Disconnect power for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect control board for damage or burn marks.
  • Check wiring harness connections.
  • Replace control board if issue persists.
F1
Touchpad / Control Board Error
  • Clean touch panel surface.
  • Check ribbon cable connection.
  • Inspect for moisture or damage.
  • Replace UI panel if needed.
E5
Temperature Detection Fault
  • Allow unit to stabilize temperature.
  • Check oven environment ventilation.
  • Inspect temperature sensor (NTC).
  • Restart after 10–15 minutes.
F2 / E0
Keypad Disconnected
  • Check keypad ribbon cable connection.
  • Inspect connector pins for corrosion.
  • Reseat connection properly.
  • Replace keypad module if needed.
F2 / E1
Keypad Stuck / Shorted
  • Clean control panel surface.
  • Check for stuck or damaged buttons.
  • Inspect membrane keypad condition.
  • Replace UI panel if necessary.
F3 / E0
Oven Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Test oven temperature sensor (NTC).
  • Inspect wiring for breaks or burns.
  • Check resistance values.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
F3 / E2
Overheating in Bake Mode
  • Check heating element relay operation.
  • Inspect temperature sensor calibration.
  • Ensure proper internal airflow.
  • Replace thermostat or control board if needed.
F3 / E3
Overheating in Self-Clean Mode
  • Allow oven to cool completely.
  • Check thermal fuse condition.
  • Inspect cooling fan operation.
  • Service required if repeated issue occurs.
E1
High Temperature Sensor Error
  • Inspect sensor wiring harness.
  • Check for loose connections.
  • Test sensor resistance.
  • Replace sensor if readings are abnormal.
F4
Bake / Broil Element Failure
  • Inspect heating element for visible damage.
  • Test continuity of bake/broil elements.
  • Check relay output from control board.
  • Replace heating element if faulty.
E2
Heating Relay Stuck ON/OFF
  • Inspect relay on control board.
  • Check for burnt PCB traces.
  • Test voltage output.
  • Replace board if relay is defective.
F4 / E1
Meat Probe Malfunction
  • Check probe connection jack.
  • Inspect probe cable for damage.
  • Test continuity of probe sensor.
  • Replace probe if faulty.
E3
Probe Not Detected
  • Reinsert probe securely.
  • Check jack for dirt or corrosion.
  • Inspect wiring to control board.
  • Replace probe or jack if needed.
F5 / E0
Door Latch Switch Error
  • Inspect door latch alignment.
  • Check door switch wiring.
  • Ensure door closes fully.
  • Replace switch if necessary.
F5 / E1
Self-Clean Latch Problem
  • Allow oven to cool completely.
  • Check latch motor operation.
  • Inspect mechanical lock assembly.
  • Replace latch motor if stuck.
E4
Door Not Fully Closed
  • Close oven door firmly.
  • Inspect hinge alignment.
  • Check obstruction inside oven cavity.
  • Replace latch assembly if needed.
PF
Power Failure Reset Required
  • Check household power supply.
  • Inspect breaker and outlet.
  • Unplug for 5–10 minutes.
  • Restart oven system.
E6
Low Voltage or Power Instability
  • Check voltage supply stability.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Inspect wiring connections.
  • Call electrician if issue persists.
E7
Control Board Communication Error
  • Inspect wiring harness connections.
  • Reset power cycle.
  • Check for moisture inside panel.
  • Replace PCB if needed.
F0
Main Electronic Control Failure
  • Disconnect power for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect PCB for burn marks.
  • Check wiring harness connections.
  • Replace control board if required.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Whirlpool oven / range show system messages or error codes?
Whirlpool oven / range system messages usually indicate issues with temperature sensors, heating elements, door latch system, control board faults, ignition problems (for gas models), or wiring and communication errors. Restart the unit and inspect power supply, door closure, and heating components if the message persists.
Why does my Whirlpool oven / range stop working suddenly?
Sudden shutdowns may be caused by thermal protection activation, power supply issues, faulty control board, tripped thermal fuse, or overheating. Turn off the unit, let it cool, then restart after checking power connection and ventilation.
Why is my Whirlpool oven / range not heating properly?
Heating issues may be caused by a faulty bake or broil element, igniter failure (gas models), temperature sensor malfunction, or relay/control board problems. Inspect heating components and verify proper power or gas supply.
Why is my oven heating unevenly?
Uneven heating may be caused by failing heating elements, poor sensor calibration, blocked airflow inside cavity, or faulty thermostat. Check elements and ensure proper oven rack placement.
Why is my Whirlpool oven temperature inaccurate?
Temperature inaccuracies are commonly caused by a faulty oven sensor (RTD), wiring issues, or calibration drift in the control board. Test sensor resistance and replace if readings are out of range.
Why does my oven show overheating warnings?
Overheating warnings may be triggered by a faulty thermostat, stuck relay, blocked airflow, or sensor failure. Allow the oven to cool and inspect temperature control components.
Why is my Whirlpool gas oven not igniting?
Ignition failure may be caused by a weak igniter, gas supply interruption, faulty safety valve, or control board issues. Check gas flow and igniter glow during startup.
Why does my oven click but not light?
This usually indicates a faulty igniter, blocked burner, or gas flow issue. Inspect burner ports and igniter condition.
Why does my Whirlpool oven / range not start?
Ensure the door is fully closed and latch is engaged. A faulty door switch or safety lock can prevent operation. Also check power supply and control settings.
Why does my oven show a door lock error?
Door lock errors occur when the latch motor is stuck, misaligned, or not detected by the control board. Inspect lock assembly and wiring.
Why does my Whirlpool oven / range show a general system error?
General system errors are usually caused by temporary control board glitches, wiring communication faults, or sensor interruptions. A full power reset often resolves the issue.
Why is my oven display not responding?
Display issues may be caused by control board failure, keypad malfunction, or power supply instability. Check connections and reset power.
Why does my Whirlpool oven / range keep restarting or losing power?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, loose wiring, tripped breaker, or failing control board. Avoid extension cords and ensure stable electrical supply.
Why does my oven not turn on at all?
Check circuit breaker, fuse, power outlet, and internal thermal fuse. A failed control board or wiring issue may also prevent startup.
Why is my Whirlpool oven / range fan noisy or not working?
Fan issues may be caused by motor wear, debris buildup, or wiring faults. Inspect cooling and convection fans for obstruction or damage.
Why is my oven making unusual noises?
Noises may come from fan motors, loose heating elements, or expanding metal during heating cycles. Inspect internal components for movement or damage.
How do I reset Whirlpool oven / range system messages?
Turn off the unit, switch off power at the breaker for 5–10 minutes, then restart. This clears temporary faults and resets the control system.
When should I service my Whirlpool oven / range?
Service is recommended if heating fails, error codes repeat, ignition problems persist, door lock issues continue, or the unit shuts down unexpectedly.
Important: Whirlpool oven / range system messages are designed to protect key components such as heating elements, igniters, temperature sensors, door lock system, cooling fans, wiring, and control board. If problems continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
