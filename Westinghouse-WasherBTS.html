Westinghouse Washer Error Codes

Complete Westinghouse Washer Guide

This guide explains all Westinghouse Washer error codes (CH series) with simple troubleshooting steps to help you identify and fix common issues safely before calling a technician.

E1
Water Inlet Issue
  • Check water supply valve is fully open.
  • Inspect inlet hose for kinks or blockage.
  • Clean inlet filter screen.
  • Verify household water pressure is sufficient.
E2
Drain Pump Failure
  • Inspect drain hose for blockage or kinks.
  • Clean pump filter and trap.
  • Check pump impeller for debris.
  • Replace drain pump if not operating.
E3
Door Lock Error
  • Ensure door is fully closed and latched.
  • Inspect door strike and lock alignment.
  • Check wiring to door lock mechanism.
  • Replace door lock assembly if faulty.
E4
Motor / Drum Drive Failure
  • Check motor wiring connections.
  • Inspect belt, pulley, and drum rotation.
  • Test motor resistance and operation.
  • Replace motor or drive components if needed.
E5
Water Overflow Detected
  • Turn off water supply immediately.
  • Inspect inlet valve for sticking open.
  • Check water level pressure sensor system.
  • Drain excess water and reset unit.
E6
Temperature Sensor Fault
  • Inspect temperature sensor wiring harness.
  • Test sensor resistance with multimeter.
  • Check for corrosion or loose connector pins.
  • Replace sensor if readings are abnormal.
E7
Heater Element Fault
  • Check heating element continuity.
  • Inspect wiring for burn marks or looseness.
  • Verify control board output to heater.
  • Replace heating element if defective.
E8
Unbalanced Load Detected
  • Redistribute laundry evenly inside drum.
  • Reduce load size if overloaded.
  • Check suspension springs and shock absorbers.
  • Run spin cycle again after adjustment.
E9
Control Board Communication Error
  • Reset power by unplugging for 5–10 minutes.
  • Inspect wiring harness between modules.
  • Check for moisture or corrosion on PCB.
  • Replace control board if issue persists.
E10
Pressure Sensor Fault
  • Check air pressure tube for blockage or leaks.
  • Inspect pressure sensor wiring connection.
  • Clean air trap chamber.
  • Replace pressure sensor if defective.
E11
Overheating Protection Triggered
  • Allow washer to cool down completely.
  • Check ventilation around appliance.
  • Inspect motor for overload condition.
  • Restart after system resets.
E12
Power Supply / Voltage Error
  • Check household power stability.
  • Avoid using extension cords.
  • Inspect power plug and socket condition.
  • Use voltage stabilizer if needed.
E13
Water Level Sensor Error
  • Inspect water level sensor connection.
  • Check pressure hose for cracks or blockage.
  • Test sensor response during fill cycle.
  • Replace sensor if faulty.
E14
Door Switch Fault
  • Ensure door closes firmly and locks.
  • Inspect door switch alignment.
  • Test switch continuity.
  • Replace door switch if defective.
E15
Drain System Blockage
  • Clean drain filter and pump area.
  • Check drain hose for kinks or clogging.
  • Inspect sink drain connection.
  • Remove debris from pump impeller.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why does my Westinghouse washer show system messages or error codes?
Westinghouse washer system messages usually indicate issues with water inlet, drainage, unbalanced load, door lock system, motor faults, pressure sensor errors, or control board communication failures. Restart the washer and inspect related components if the message persists.
Why does my Westinghouse washer stop working suddenly?
Sudden shutdowns may be caused by power interruptions, overheating protection, water supply failure, blocked drain system, or internal electronic faults. Turn off the washer, allow it to reset, then check water and power supply before restarting.
Why is my Westinghouse washer not filling with water?
Check that the water tap is fully open, inlet hose is not kinked, and inlet filter is clean. Low water pressure or a faulty inlet valve may also prevent proper filling.
Why is my washer showing water inlet error?
This error usually indicates restricted water flow or inlet valve malfunction. Clean filters, inspect hoses, and ensure stable water pressure.
Why is my Westinghouse washer not draining water?
Drain issues are commonly caused by clogged filter, blocked hose, or faulty drain pump. Clean the pump filter and check for obstructions in the drain line.
Why does my washer leave water inside the drum?
Standing water may indicate pump failure, hose blockage, or drainage system restriction. Inspect and clean all drain components.
Why does my Westinghouse washer not start?
Ensure the door is fully closed and locked. Check power supply and control settings. A faulty door lock switch may also prevent operation.
Why does my washer show a door error?
Door errors occur when the latch is not engaged or the door switch is faulty. Close the door firmly and inspect the locking mechanism.
Why is my Westinghouse washer not spinning properly?
Spin issues may be caused by unbalanced load, overloaded drum, drainage problems, or motor faults. Redistribute laundry and check drain system.
Why does my washer stop mid-cycle?
Mid-cycle stopping may be caused by water supply interruption, overload protection, motor overheating, or control board errors.
Why is my Westinghouse washer overfilling or showing water level errors?
Check the pressure sensor hose for blockage, leaks, or disconnection. A faulty water level sensor may also cause incorrect readings.
Why does my washer show sensor errors?
Sensor errors are usually caused by faulty pressure switches, wiring issues, or control board communication faults. Inspect connections and test sensors.
Why is my Westinghouse washer leaking water?
Leaks may come from hose connections, door seal, detergent drawer, or drain pump. Inspect all seals and tighten loose fittings.
Why does my Westinghouse washer keep restarting or losing power?
This may be caused by unstable voltage, loose wiring, faulty outlet, or control board issues. Avoid extension cords and check power stability.
Why does my washer show a general system error?
General errors usually indicate temporary control board glitches or communication failures. A full reset often resolves the issue.
How do I reset Westinghouse washer system messages?
Turn off the washer, unplug it for 5–10 minutes, then reconnect power. This resets the control system and clears temporary errors.
When should I service my Westinghouse washer?
Service is recommended if errors persist, draining fails repeatedly, spin cycle stops, leaks continue, or the washer repeatedly shuts down.
Important: Westinghouse washer system messages are designed to protect key components such as the motor, drain pump, inlet valve, pressure sensor, door lock system, wiring harness, and control board. If issues continue after troubleshooting, disconnect power and contact a qualified appliance technician.
